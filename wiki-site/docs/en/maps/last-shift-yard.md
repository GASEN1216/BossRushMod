# Last Shift Yard

A railway marshalling yard that stopped running years ago, though freight still comes and goes at night: a train every two minutes, and train 8 is the last train; once it leaves, the yard closes. The switch in the suspended cab decides whether the train stops at the west or the east platform, and the escorts follow the cargo, so throwing the switch means choosing where the fight happens. Every train that stops can be signed for, and the more you sign, the harder the escorts come. The yard also has one ticket inspector who never went off shift: Yu Xiang is still punching tickets for a last train that was cancelled long ago.

## Setting out

- The first time you're back at base, there's a "last-train ticketing resumed" notice by the departures. After that, **Jeff** offers the quest "The Last Train's Still Taking Tickets": find the ticket inspector Yu Xiang in the warehouse district, talk to her, and report back to Jeff. There's no reward; it just shows you the way.
- The way in is in the official **Hidden Warehouse** map: outside the rails of the second gantry crane in the west yard there is a ticket desk and a shuttle truck, with Yu Xiang in a peaked cap and leather jacket standing beside it. Until you have visited the yard, the warehouse M map marks the "Last train ticket desk", and when you walk up she calls out "Last train. Tickets, please."
- Yu Xiang is on the neutral side: warehouse enemies leave her alone, and she won't fight for you. Stand in front of her and choose "Talk" to hear about the station.
- You can only board during a warehouse raid that is still going. "Board the last train" opens a confirmation first; once you confirm, she brings the ticket punch down and you transfer. Your backpack comes with you, and the run isn't settled until you extract from the yard, which is the only way back to base. The ticket is free, and each run she quietly slips you a bandage.
- You can't board while a mode, a Sky Islands trip or a Tidegate trip is running.
- The loading screen stops at "Click to continue", and you land outside the south crew room.

## The first-train demonstration

- On your first visit, the first train waits until you stand **behind the south rail**. From there you watch the engine smash the hand cart off the track. If you stay away from the rail for more than a minute, the first train leaves anyway. The first train only whistles for a few seconds before it comes in, and it stands longer than usual (90 s), so you have time to reach either platform and sign.
- The switch is yours once the demonstration is over. Climb the ramp to the **suspended cab** and use "Throw the switch".
- From the second train on, **a head-on hit from the engine kills you outright**. The cars are solid and block both people and bullets; brushing against one only pushes you aside.

## The switch and the timetable

- After you throw the switch, the yard tannoy says which line the freight takes and where the escorts are heading, and green arrows on the ground point to that platform for 8 seconds. The west platform is under a shed with guards close by; the east platform means crossing open ground.
- A train comes about every two minutes: 15 s whistle, 22 s arrival, 40 s unloading, 16 s departure, 25 s line clear. The small text in the top-right corner shows which platform, which step, how many seconds are left and how many trains remain before the yard closes. Once a train is in, the route is locked until the line clears.
- A train can be ordinary **freight**; a **slow train** (it stands 58 s and the escorts all turn up); **heavy freight** (one more car, a longer shadow to hide behind); or an **express** (no stop, no unloading, through in 13 s with a lethal engine; keep off the track).
- **The engine hits escorts too**: an escort caught head-on by an arriving or departing engine dies on the spot; a squad leader loses 60% of their health and the chief escort 30%. It counts as your kill. Lead your pursuers onto the track and wait for the train.
- While a train is arriving or departing, you can cross from the centre line to the east platform with the cars blocking the view.
- The **last train** is train 8 (the demonstration train counts as train 1). It is always heavy freight and always stops. Once it has stopped, the brake van lowers its rear step on the platform side and an extraction ring appears there: stand in it until the bar fills and you ride out. When the train pulls away, the ring moves north with it for a while, so you can still finish by running alongside.
- **Yard closed**: after the last train leaves, no more trains come and the switch lever is wired down. The west service gate and the east escort gate are padlocked and the railcar stops running, leaving only the north road. As soon as the last train pulls in, a checkpoint goes up on the north road (an escort sergeant and three escorts). Once the yard is closed, escorts who aren't already fighting sweep toward you every 10 seconds, and replacements come fastest.

## Sealed cargo and looting

- Each run has a manifest: medical, repair or precision. **Every train that stops can be signed for once**: follow the HUD objective to the sealed cabinet on the side where the train stops.
- The first cabinet holds 8 items: the first two are quality 4 or better, the rest are odds and ends matching the manifest (quality 2-4). Every later cabinet holds 4 items and only the first slot is guaranteed: its chance of quality 5 is 40% on the second cabinet, +10% for each one after, up to 70%.
- **The premium platform changes every train**: the tannoy only tells you where this train's premium tag is when it pulls in ("There's a premium tag on the wagon", or "The premium tag was on the other platform's dispatch sheet"). Signing at the premium platform: the first cabinet has 9 items with an even chance of a quality 5 first slot; later cabinets have 5 items and +20% on that chance (up to 90%).
- **Every train is a fight, and the more you sign, the harder it gets**: apart from the demonstration train and expresses, every train that stops drops a wave of escorts from the rear of the brake van on the platform side: 3 to start, 1 more for each cabinet you've signed (up to 5), plus 1 on the last train who guards the step. From two signed cabinets on, one of them is a squad leader. Replacements arrive every 16 seconds, 3 seconds sooner per cabinet signed (8 s at the fastest). If you're within 25 m of the rear of the train, they get off at the north end of the platform instead.
- Train escorts don't count toward the run's total, and up to 10 can be out at once. When a new wave gets off, anyone from earlier waves who is more than 65 m from you and not fighting leaves with the train to make room.
- When the next train stops on the same side, whatever you left in the previous cabinet is hauled back aboard. Empty it before the next train arrives (it waits while you have the loot screen open).
- If the cargo is incomplete, you will be asked to try again without using up that train's signature.
- A stopped train drops a batch of crates at its platform.
- The west platform, the bond store and the east platform each have a risk cabinet. Opening one sets off the store alarm, and nearby escorts hurry back.

## Extraction

- **North road**: always open; there's a checkpoint once the last train pulls in.
- **West line service gate**: opens when no train is on the west line; padlocked once the yard closes.
- **East platform escort gate**: opens after a train unloads at the east platform; padlocked once the yard closes.
- **Railcar shelter**: ring the railcar bell at the shelter first; the railcar arrives about 14 s later. Nearby patrols can hear the bell too. It stops running once the yard closes.
- **Last train's brake-van step**: only while the last train stands and pulls out; it moves with the train.

Closed exits have no ground ring. Step into one and you'll see why it's shut. The countdown works like the base game's extraction points: it pauses while your backpack is open or the game is paused, and resets if you leave the circle.

## The escorts, the cargo guards and the chief escort

- Escorts patrol, hold posts and follow the freight to whichever platform it stops at. Up to 10 patrols and post guards are out at once (roaming patrols are capped at 5, 7 once the yard closes), 40-46 over a whole run, and the ones you take down are gradually replaced; train escorts are counted separately (see above).
- **Cargo guards**: every place with crates has a crew watching it: the west unloading shed, the east platform, the inspection shed, the weigh shed by the repair shed, the bond store door, the escort yard, the east crew room, under the north bridge and the returns stack in the northeast. The canteen table and the relay stock have no crates; each has an even chance of a crew resting there each run. A crew only takes up its post once you're about seventy metres away. Cargo guards are counted separately: up to 10 at once, 80 over a run.
  - The crews by the risk cabinets (inspection shed, bond store, northeast returns) and the checkpoint are always awake on guard; at the other places with crates, each run it's an even chance between standing guard and resting in a circle.
  - Crews on guard stay awake near their post and shoot when they see you.
  - Resting crews can see you within about 11 m in front of them if no wall or steam is in the way, or if you get within 3.5 m. They can hear you: footsteps (walking while aiming down sights makes none), gunfire within 30 m, a grenade landing. The store alarm going off within 30 m wakes them too. When you get within a dozen-odd metres they chat for a moment; they haven't noticed you yet.
  - If one of them gets shot or goes down, the whole crew wakes up and comes after you.
  - Ringing the railcar bell brings a resting crew within 50 m of the bell over to look. After about 35 seconds they walk back and settle down again, unless they spot you on the way. The store alarm, the repair shed buzzer and incoming trains don't move them.
  - A crew you haven't fought stands down once you're more than 85 m away, and is back on post when you return.
  - A place whose crew you wiped out stays empty until the next train stops on that side (west or east): a squad gets off the brake van to restaff it, so going back there later means another fight.
  - The important places are led by a squad leader; see the next section.
- The **chief escort** stands guard behind the sealed cabinet on the platform where the train stops, and has 1000 health. He appears behind the cabinet once the first stopping train is assigned a platform. Later switch changes send him walking to the other platform, though he will still engage enemies on the way. An ordinary escort watches the bond store door. He fights with his gun as usual, and once he's spotted you he alternates two moves:
  - **Line closed**: he stands still, raises a red flag, gives two short whistles and lays a yellow-and-black striped lane on the ground toward you (about 2.6 m wide, up to 40 m long, cut short by a wall, cover or a train car pulling in). About 1.5 seconds later he rakes it for 1.5 seconds, 10 damage every 0.3 s. The lane doesn't turn to follow you: step sideways out of it, or get behind a wall or a car. Service steam can stop him starting one, but it won't stop a lane that's already laid.
  - **Track torpedoes**: one long whistle, then he throws 2 torpedoes at your feet (a little ahead of where you're running) and to one side. Each lands with a red glow on the ground and goes off about 1.3 seconds later (2.2 m radius, 25 damage, walls block it). Step out of the red ring.
  - At half health he gives three short whistles and switches to a hand lamp. From then on he lays two lanes at once (the second angled toward the side you're moving to; stand between them or get into cover) with only about 1.2 seconds' warning, throws 3 torpedoes in a row that go off after about 1.05 seconds, and waits less between moves.
  - The lane drives you into cover; the torpedoes flush you out of it. On return visits he sometimes rides in on the brake van and jumps off at the platform.
- Down the chief escort to take the **Escort Steel Stamp** from him.

## Squad leaders

The three squad leaders aren't bosses (no boss health bar, no special drop), but they're tougher than escorts (about 2.6 times the ordinary health) and each has a move nobody else uses. They lead cargo guards, the brake van brings them once you've signed two cabinets, and the last train always carries one.

- **Signal Foreman** (carries a red lamp): when he can see you, a red beam reaches out from his lamp. Hold it on you for about 1.4 seconds and he has **called you out**: every resting or guarding crew within 40 m wakes up, nearby escorts who aren't already fighting rush you, and a red ring follows your feet for 6 seconds. Get behind a wall, a train car or service steam and the beam breaks; he tries again after about 5 seconds, and once he has called you out he waits about 16 seconds. Take him first.
- **Shunting Master** (raises a red flag): he stands still, raises the flag, gives one long whistle and lays a yellow-and-black warning strip from his feet toward you (up to 34 m, cut short by a wall, cover or a train car pulling in). About 1.1 seconds later he lets a runaway hand cart go down it at 15 m/s: it does 35 damage to you (armour applies) and 60 to any escort it hits, his own crew included. Step out of the strip, or stand behind his crew and let the cart do the work. About once every 8 seconds.
- **Escort Sergeant**: when he can see you, he shoves an inspection trolley out in front of him as cover (it blocks bullets, is taken away after 22 seconds, and you can use it too). If you stay behind cover for about 2.5 seconds, he shouts "Push up!" and up to 5 escorts within 25 m flank you from both sides. Take him down and the flanking stops.

## The stamp and the sealed side cabinet

- After a safe extraction, bring the stamp along on your **next** trip and use it once on the **sealed side cabinet** in the bond store (the first slot is a valuable of quality 4 or better). Opening the cabinet uses the stamp up. A stamp picked up on the current run won't open it.
- If you already carry one when you down the chief again, you get his private stock instead of a second stamp.
- Dying loses the stamp you carry.

## The ticket nobody came for

- There are three clues in the yard: **the ticket punch in the cab**, **the old tape in the repair shed** and **the returns ledger in the bond store**. The ones you haven't found are marked on the M map; once you've read one, "Last ticket n/3" shows in the top-right corner. Each time you bring one out, go back and Talk to Yu Xiang at the warehouse; she'll tell you what it means to her, then where to look next.
- When all three come together in one run (clues you took out on earlier extractions count), you get the **Last Shift Ticket Stub**. Take it out and give it to Yu Xiang: you can hand it over when you Talk to her, and boarding also hands it over automatically. The story is added to your notes.
- Clues only count once you extract safely. Clues read on a run where you die are not kept, and you lose the stub you carry.
- Once the story is finished, Yu Xiang tells you about the next train and where its premium tag goes every time she punches your ticket, and has dispatch set the switch for it: the switch is already set when you land, though you can still go up to the cab and change it. The manifest board also shows the next train's premium platform.
- The story is optional and doesn't affect looting or extraction.

## Items

- **Escort Steel Stamp** (500117): dropped by the chief escort; opens the sealed side cabinet once on your next trip, then it's gone.
- **Last Shift Ticket Stub** (500118): from reading all three clues; give it to Yu Xiang at the warehouse ticket desk to finish the story. Can't be sold.

Neither item appears in random reward pools.
