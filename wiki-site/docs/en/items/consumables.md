# Consumables

## Birthday Cake

- **Source**: the normal Wish Fountain reward pool; a one-time save gift during real-world December (not renewed each year) / **Affinity**: +150 to Dingdang or Yuori
- **Eating**: restores 100 energy, no water; also applies the vanilla Happy buff when that buff is available

Second only to Diamond Ring (+500) for raw Affinity gain. If you get one, **give it to whoever you're courting**.

## Calming Drops

Yuori's hand-prepared herbal drops. Clears treatable debuffs such as bleeding, fractures and poison.

- **Source**: 5 free at Yuori Affinity Lv.3
- Stack 20 / Use time 2.5s
- **Effect**: Removes treatable debuffs; does not remove the Phantom Witch's **Ghost Curse**
- **Use condition**: At least one treatable debuff is active; Ghost Curse alone does not allow use

Clutch item in hard modes. See NPC Items for details.

## Cold Quench Fluid

The Reforge system's key material. **Locks one stat during Reforging** so it doesn't change.

- **Source**: Dingdang Lv.4 reward / Dingdang's shop / daily gift from married Dingdang

See NPC Items and Reforge System for details.

## Homecoming Bento

A lunch box packed by Qinghe: rice topped with homecoming greens and a little paper pinwheel.

- **Source**: occasionally found in Sky Islands crates; once Qinghe has her planting record back, also cooked at her stove from Greenear Sheaf ×4 + Driftwood ×1
- **Effect**: restores 35 energy and 20 water, plus 15 health (still edible at full health)
- **Homecoming meal**: once Qinghe has her planting record back, eating it on the Sky Islands counts as her homecoming meal: a little more max health and running speed for this raid, shared with her own meal (if you have already had one this raid, it is just lunch)
- Stack 10 / Use time 2.5s

See Sky Islands · Qinglan Archipelago.

## Starmoss Salve

A salve Miantai boils down from star moss; it goes on cool.

- **Source**: occasionally found in Sky Islands Voyage Supplies and Starworks Cache crates; also ground at Miantai's mortar from Cloudmoss Fiber ×4 + Windcrystal Shard ×1
- **Effect**: restores 40 health; no payment to Miantai and no waiting on her cooldown. Rubbed on anywhere on the isles it **stops cloud-gnat itching**, and for about 90 seconds afterwards new bites will not itch, so **you do not have to wait until you itch**; applying it before you head out at night counts just the same
- Stack 10 / Use time 3s

See Sky Islands · Qinglan Archipelago.

## Wind Lantern

A small lantern on a driftwood frame with a cloudmoss paper shade.

- **Source**: made at Fuzhou's dock workbench on the Sky Islands (or the dock device) from Driftwood ×2 + Cloudmoss Fiber ×1
- **Effect**: on the Qinglan isles, a warm light follows you overhead for about 4 minutes of game time; **in a breeze** the chill does not build and slowly fades, **in a gale** (night bridges, the boardwalk before the storm) the flame gutters and the chill still builds at half speed. Using another only refills the timer
- **Cloud gnats at night**: the light **draws more** of them, but the ones within 6 m in it only circle the shade: **they neither bite nor dodge bullets**; with 20 seconds left you are told how many are still circling, and the moment it goes out they are back on your neck (clear them with the remedy-smoke fan or a shotgun while it burns)
- **Also**: the windcrystal lamp at the Homecoming Bell Court hangs one
- **Limits**: usable on the Sky Islands only (the use button is greyed out elsewhere); goes out when you leave
- Stack 5 / Use time 1.2s

## Windward Incense

A cake of pounded cloudmoss and greenear whose smoke is warm.

- **Source**: made at Qinghe's stove (or the terrace garden) or Miantai's mortar (or the Hanging Root Wood note "a mailbag in the roots") from Cloudmoss Fiber ×2 + Greenear Sheaf ×2
- **Effect**: on the Qinglan isles, about 5 minutes of game time safe from any wind (gales included), with +15% stamina recovery
- **Also**: the windcrystal lamp at Mirrorwater Temple burns one in its censer
- **Limits**: usable on the Sky Islands only; ends when you leave
- Stack 5 / Use time 2s

---

## Qinglan Charm

A small charm on a brass backing, set with a windcrystal shard and a pinch of stardust.

- **Source**: made at Fuzhou's dock workbench from Brass Scrap ×3 + Windcrystal Shard ×2 + Stardust ×1
- **Effect**: for this raid, **35% less damage from the Windeater's storm pulses**, +10% max health (only the newly added portion is topped up) and +10% stamina recovery
- **Limits**: usable on the Sky Islands only; one per raid (the second one's use button is greyed out); ends when you leave
- Stack 3 / Use time 1.5s

---

## Windcrystal Gnat Zapper

A windcrystal wick Weibai tuned half a note lower, caged in brass by Fuzhou.

- **Source**: made at Fuzhou's dock workbench on the Sky Islands (or the dock device) from Qinglan Windcrystal ×1 + Brass Scrap ×3, two per batch; **only once two windcrystal lamps are lit on the isles**
- **Effect**: on the Qinglan isles, set it at your feet for about 5 minutes of game time: its hum draws cloud gnats in from 12 m and zaps one within 3.2 m about every 0.7 s (you can see the arc); up to two at once. **Use it to hold an area**; for the swarm on your face, use the Remedy-Smoke Fan
- **Limits**: usable on the Sky Islands only; with two already burning the use button is greyed out, so none are wasted; goes out when you leave
- Stack 4 / Use time 1.2s

See "Gathering, crafting, the isles' lights and the night wind" and "Cloud gnats at night" on Sky Islands · Qinglan Archipelago.
