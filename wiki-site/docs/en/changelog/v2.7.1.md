# v2.7.1

## Release date
- 2026-11-27
- A fix patch for Dingdang's Reforge: maxed weapons can roll Max again, every stat is guaranteed to hit its best limit within 100 Reforges, and the Reforge panel only looks at the item itself. The art for the Duckov Codex, achievements, the Duck King Campaign, Fate Echo, the Black Market Duck Cup and Sky Island has also been redrawn in the vanilla style.

## Balance

- **Reforge pity**: each stat keeps its own count of how many Reforges in a row missed its best limit (Max where higher is better, Min for stats like recoil where lower is better). The longer the streak, the better the odds that the next Reforge lands right on it, slowly at first and then faster: about 25% on the 50th, guaranteed on the 100th. The count restarts once it lands; stats locked with Cold Quench Fluid are skipped and their count stays put. Pity always gives the good end, whatever your benefit tendency, and on pity alone it comes about once every 27 Reforges.

## Improvements

- **The Reforge panel only looks at the item**: bonuses or penalties from attachments on a gun no longer count toward the numbers, the change or the Max / Min tags in the Reforge panel. The item details in your inventory still show the real value with attachments.
- **Redrawn art**: Duckov Codex portraits, achievement icons, Duck King Campaign chapter posters, the Fate Echo and Black Market Duck Cup confirmation pages, Sky Island resident dialogue and area banners, and the wiki site icons have all been redrawn in the vanilla style. The Nightmare clear achievement now has an icon, and the four no-damage achievements carry a glass bell so they are easy to tell from the regular ones.

## Fixes

- **Reforge never rolled Max**: after the last version switched to rolling from factory values, hitting the cap was close to impossible, and stats whose factory value is something like 0.35 always fell just short (the 0.525 cap was rounded to 0.52). Now a roll in the top magnitude band goes straight to the cap or floor and shows Max / Min.
- **Attachments threw off Max / Min**: with attachments on a gun, the Reforge panel counted their bonuses as part of the reforge change, so maxed stats lost their Max tag and unmaxed ones sometimes showed it. Only the item's own values count now.
- **One-step changes went unmarked**: a stat like a factory 0.02 rolled up to its 0.03 cap showed no change and lost its Max tag. It shows normally now.
- **Flight totem costs crept up**: acceleration time and the three stamina costs on flight totems are better when lower, so Reforge tendency and pity now push them down, the same as recoil. Tendency used to push them up.

::: tip
This version keeps your existing saves and config and adds no new items. Pity progress is stored on each item; gear reforged before this update starts counting from 0 and keeps its current values and Max / Min tags.
:::
