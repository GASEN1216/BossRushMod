# v2.7.0

## Release date
- 2026-11-27
- This version adds the **War Writ**: set your own lineup, challenge and march at the arena signpost. Hard and Nightmare come with full rule sets, weather shifts, calamities and Boss affixes, with bigger rewards to match, and Standard and Hard can be raised level by level. The whole UI moves to an ink style, and there are also constellations at the wish fountain, soul offerings at the Relic Nest, gift notes in the affinity tab and a new totem, the Hushbell Ward. See the `Standard BossRush` page for the full rules.

## New

- **War Writ**: the arena signpost's main option is now "War Writ". Pick a lineup (A Snap, Getting Interesting, Infinite Hell), a challenge (Relaxed, Standard, Hard, Nightmare) and a march (Short, Long, Full) in the three rows on the left; the notice on the right lists that challenge's rules and rewards, and **Swear** starts the run. It reopens on whatever you picked last time. The signpost's old easter egg has moved into a sub-option.
- **Four challenges**: Relaxed has 3 graces (weaker Bosses, +50% healing, longer rest between waves) and halves the clear crate; Standard is the same as before; Hard adds 6 rules and Nightmare 10. On Nightmare, Bosses get ×2.5 HP and ×1.75 damage, plus Blinded, Cut Supply, Blood Debt and the closing fire wall of City Ablaze. From Hard up, clear crate items start at tier 6 and Bosses with affixes drop extra loot.
- **Omens, calamities and Boss affixes**: on Hard and Nightmare the weather turns (Blizzard, Thunderstorm, Blood Moon, Endless Night, Downpour), and calamities that hurt both sides strike now and then. There are nine, including Meteor Rain, Lightning, Miasma and Ink Tide. The weather cuts both ways: in a Thunderstorm, Lightning hits Bosses twice as hard; under a Blood Moon, fallen Bosses drop 1 extra item; in Endless Night, enemies can't see well either and react more slowly. Bosses carry affixes of four kinds (clone, guard, motion, death), shown before their names, and every wave brings a hex such as Echo, Festering or Exposed. Every effect is spelled out in the wiki.
- **Short and Long March**: 12 or 24 waves, ending on a strong headliner Boss, with the clear crate scaled by wave count. Full Campaign is unchanged and runs every enabled Boss.
- **Raise**: a row of seals sits under the challenge cards. Once you've picked a challenge you can raise it level by level, carrying every level below the one you pick. Standard has 5 levels and plays close to Hard when maxed; Hard has 8 and plays close to Nightmare. Each level is one rule from the next challenge up, ordered from mild to harsh, and each adds 5% to the clear crate's item count and to Hell cash. Each challenge remembers the level you last picked, and the highest level you've cleared is marked **Best**. Relaxed and Nightmare can't be raised.
- **Wish fountain constellations**: a dome of twelve constellations now sits above the wish panel. Light one and this draw leans toward that category. When you open the panel, other players' wishes rise on paper lanterns at both sides; the switch in the top-right corner puts them out.
- **Soul offerings**: under Growth on the nest page, spend that bloodline's souls (2 souls per XP) to lift a hatchling straight to the next level, once per hatchling per in-game day.
- **Gift notes**: every NPC in the affinity tab now has a gift notes section. Each gift that hits something they love or hate gets written down; entries you haven't found only show how many are left.
- **Names up close**: walk within 8 m of Dingdang, Yu Zhi, the courier or the residents of Sky Island and Tidegate and their name fades in above their head, then fades out again as you leave.
- **Beacon smoke on the M map**: in Standard, Infinite Hell, From Scratch, Faction War and Fate Echo, the map circles roughly where the nearest enemy Bosses are, up to 8. If the fight stalls, the circles shrink step by step and finally lock onto their live positions.
- **New totem, the Hushbell Ward**: trade 30 seashells for it once the Silent Bell Keeper reaches affinity level 5. It wraps you in an 80-point bronze bell shield that takes hits first. It refills at 20 points per second starting 5 seconds after the last hit, and waits at least 9 seconds after it breaks.
- **New item**: Hushbell Ward (500220).

## Balance

- **Dingdang's reforge toned down**: every reforge now rolls from the gear's factory values instead of building on the last result, and each stat lands within 50%-150% of its factory value (stats that start at 0 within ±0.25, armor within ±1). Gear that was rolled past the new range is held to the new cap when you load.
- **No random mutators in War Writ runs**: in Standard BossRush and Infinite Hell, the challenge you pick is the only difficulty; random mutators are no longer rolled on top. From Scratch, Faction War and Blood Hunt still roll them.
- **Ratswarm**: half as likely to show up, and never in the same run as Giants.

## Improvements

- **Ink UI**: the mod's panels, buttons and confirm dialogs now use ink backgrounds with gold edges and settle in slowly when they open. The Adventurer's Journal is now an ink scroll with illustrations.
- **Zombie crowds spread out**: from wave 5 on in Zombie Mode, big crowds no longer twitch and bunch up around the spawn; they fan out and rush you.
- **Dingdang waits longer**: after a chat, a reforge or closing the shop, Dingdang stays put for 2.5 minutes, so you can dig through your backpack for the next piece without Dingdang wandering off. Talking again works as usual.
- **Entering Faction War or Blood Hunt**: if you aren't fully stripped down, you're told exactly what's still on you. The raid goes ahead as normal; it just isn't that mode.
- **Settings**: the achievement panel and Fate Echo abandon hotkeys can be set to "Disabled", and ModConfig dropdown titles no longer show as code names.
- **Text**: Dingdang's, Yu Zhi's and the Sky Island residents' lines are rewritten with the same number of lines; error messages now tell you what to do next; the English text uses $ for money and the official name for seashells.
- Xiaoman, the test NPC, no longer turns up by itself in release builds.

## Fixes

- **"Coordinates Above the Clouds" couldn't be turned in**: when the HarmonyLib prerequisite sat below BossRush in the mod list, BossRush's patches didn't load and this quest couldn't be completed. BossRush now loads HarmonyLib itself at startup, so the order usually no longer matters; if it still won't turn in, move HarmonyLib above BossRush as the Installation & Activation page describes. Separately, if another mod errored in the money-changed notification while a reward was paid, the money had already arrived but was counted as failed, and every retry paid out again. Now it counts as paid as soon as the money has actually arrived. Achievement rewards are handled the same way.
- **Mod gear gone after reloading**: if the mod loaded a moment after the save, or a piece of gear wasn't recognised that time, it fell out of its slot and was lost at the next save. The mod now puts it back in its original slot from the save record (or in your backpack if it doesn't fit), and anything that still can't load goes into storage as-is and comes back next time you load. If you switch saves or levels while this is happening, nothing is put back, so gear can't end up in the wrong save.
- **Unkillable zombies**: around waves 20-30, zombies spawned stacked on top of each other; the ones on top sat above your bullets, couldn't be hit and blocked doorways. Spawn points are now spread out, and zombies can no longer stand on other characters.
- **Dragon Descendants stuck in walls**: in the Duck Cup, Faction War and Fate Echo, the Dragon Descendants summoned by the Skyburner Dragon Lord no longer get stuck in walls.
- **Sturdier startup**: an error in one part of startup no longer takes the later parts down with it. The mod finds the HarmonyLib prerequisite first, and if that copy is damaged it uses one bundled with another mod instead. The game log now gets a `[BossRush] 启动自检` (startup check) line, so if you report a problem, sending Player.log shows which part went wrong.

::: tip
Existing saves and settings carry over; the new item uses a new ID and old item IDs are unchanged. Gift notes are saved with affinity, and older saves start from "not figured out yet". If the mod doesn't load, see the Installation & Activation page.
:::
