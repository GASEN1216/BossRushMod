# v2.3.1

## Release Date
- 2026-10-07
- A comprehensive quality and stability hotfix following the release of v2.3.0 (2026-10-02), bringing usability improvements, wider mode availability for cubs, and major bug fixes.

## New

- **Boss Health Percent Slider**: a new "Boss Health Percent" slider (`BossRush_BossHealthPercent`) in mod settings and ModConfig, ranging from 10% to 200% (default 100%). Supports real-time hot-reloading in game and applies exclusively to newly spawned Bosses, multiplying independently with wave factors and gear bonuses. Regular enemies, elites, and companions are unaffected.
- **Duck Cup Custom Bet Slider**: the Black Market Duck Cup removes the fixed 20,000 cash cap and adds a "Custom amount" slider. The maximum bet dynamically calculates based on wallet balance and current match odds. All internal math is upgraded to `decimal` and `long`, freezing payout ratios when bets lock to eliminate overflow and cross-match drifts.
- **Roman Numerals for Affix Tiers**: affix levels on equipment are now standardized to Roman numerals (Ⅰ / Ⅱ / Ⅲ), cleanly updating across Chinese and English language changes without text stacking.

## Improved

- **Full Deployment Access for Cubs**: PetNest companions can now accompany you across all standard raid maps and mod game modes (including Fate Echo Mode G and Zombie Mode). The only barred mode is the Black Market Duck Cup (Mode H) spectator brawl to avoid disturbing the arena duel.
  - In Fate Echo, three-axis telemetry counts player direct damage only; companion finishing blows and kills proceed smoothly through registered Boss death settlement.
  - In Zombie Mode, kill counting only checks in-run zombie markers, so companions do not disrupt wave or reward progression.
- **Base Companion Non-Combat Safety**: while at base, companions spawn as independent non-combat entities with combat AI disabled, completely preventing accidental targeting or damage to vanilla or mod merchants.
- **Resilient Codex Kill Identity Collection**: the Duckov Codex prioritizes checking Boss-specific ability components to identify Bosses even when third-party random enemy mods alter display names, or when death triggers during preset cleanup.
- **Smoother From Scratch (Mode D) Wave Scaling**: fixed an issue where repeated multiplicative scaling caused exponential Boss HP inflation in later waves; reworked to a smooth, flat multiplicative progression (+3% per wave).

## Fixes

- Fixed Dragon Descendant collision skill in Faction War damaging friendly players: collision checks now verify mutual hostility via `Team.IsEnemy`, preventing damage to allies pursuing the same enemy.
- Fixed stuck black screen and lease leaks when Sky Islands scene loading fails: reliably releases activation barriers and decrement lease reference counts, retrying safely without overwriting character snapshots.
- Fixed an issue where IO exceptions during official game save writing left a dangling `IsSaving` lock that silently rejected all future saves; added structured exception wrapping and self-healing retries.
- Fixed inverted subscription timing during Duck King Campaign persistence recovery that could reset completed chapters back to Chapter 1; Jeff's quest client now pauses projections during read-barrier faults to prevent re-issuing completed starter quests.
- Fixed an interaction deadlock on the Chapter 6 Sign-up Stone if dialog was interrupted during the finale monologue; canceling now safely restores summon authority.
- Fixed escort Dragon Descendant spawning at half health under the 200% health setting during the Dragon Lord's second phase.
- Fixed Zombie Mode Boss spawn points falling outside playable zones by adding nearest-reachable point sampling and lifting distance-sleep restrictions.
- Cleaned up obsolete ticket IDs in fallback code, aligning strictly with constant `500001`.
