# v2.6.2

## Release date
- 2026-11-21
- Player feedback fixes: no mouse cursor in the album, the cub backpack on raid maps saying it isn't ready over and over, and the register crate not popping up on your first collectible. The wish draw is also cut back hard.

## Fixes

- **No cursor in the album**: after opening the album the mouse cursor sometimes didn't show until you opened the map. The cursor now stays visible while the album or any other mod panel is open.
- **Cub backpack on raid maps**: if you hatched an egg or collected expedition rewards at base and then headed straight out, opening the cub backpack on the map kept popping "Inventory and storage are not ready yet". The save made on your way out already includes storage, so the cub backpack now works on the map. If it really isn't ready, you only see the notice once.
- **First collectible registration**: after pressing "Register Items", the one-slot crate sometimes didn't pop up, and closing the album left you with nothing. Other mods can no longer stop that crate from opening. If it still doesn't open, you get a short notice and land back in the album; just press it again. If you can't register right now, the bottom of the album says why.

## Changes

- **Wish draw cut back**: reward quality now starts at tier 3, with odds of about 45% tier 3, 33% tier 4, 15% tier 5, 5.5% tier 6, 1.2% tier 7 and 0.3% tier 8. It used to start at tier 4, with tier 7 and above adding up to about 16%.
- **Quality unlocks with play time**: on this save, under 5 hours caps at tier 4, under 15 hours at tier 5, under 30 hours at tier 6, and under 60 hours at tier 7. Tier 8 needs 60 hours or more.
- What you write in your wish mostly decides which kind of item you get. Naming a high-tier item no longer guarantees high quality. The reward cooldown is still 4 hours.

::: tip
This version does not change the save format or any item IDs; just update over the old version.
:::
