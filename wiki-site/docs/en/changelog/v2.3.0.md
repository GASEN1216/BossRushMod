# v2.3.0

## Release Status
- **Not released yet, coming soon.** This page collects every change made after v2.2.5 (2026-08-10); all of it ships together in 2.3.0, and the date goes here on release day. A few numbers may still be tuned before then, so the individual wiki entries have the final say.

## Main Theme
- **A new name**: the mod is now called "BossRush · Qinglan Archipelago" (Chinese name: BossRush · 晴岚群岛). The online wiki lives at https://bossrushmod.pages.dev/
- **A standalone raid map**: Sky Islands · Qinglan Archipelago. Set out from the base dock with your own gear and find residents, chiefs, a main quest line, gathering and crafting, and more than one ending.
- **Two new modes**: Fate Echo remembers how you fight and builds the next wave around it; the Black Market Duck Cup puts you in the stands, betting on which of two boss teams wins the brawl, without ever stepping onto the field.
- **The mod has a story now**: Jeff at your base hands out the six Duck King Campaign chapters through the game's own quest screen, plus fourteen starter quests that walk you through the new content.
- **Plenty to do between runs**: raise cubs in the PetNest, farm and show off trophies in the Arena Backyard, and read The Duckov Daily every in-game day.
- **More reasons to hunt Bosses**: the Duckov Codex, Affix Forging and in-run random events, and the five new weapons plus the Frost and Thunder sets can finally be obtained.

## New: Sky Islands · Qinglan Archipelago

- A **standalone raid map** you reach from the base dock, not a BossRush difficulty tier. It costs no BossRush ticket and you bring your own gear and backpack. Dying works like any other raid: your things stay on the island. Extracting takes you through the familiar results screen back to base.
- The archipelago has 8 main areas and 4 side paths; the long bridges widen into relay platforms halfway across, and there are 3 return shortcuts to open. The map is the game's own map key, and areas only fill in once you have set foot on them.
- Extraction points are glowing rings on the ground: stand inside for 3 seconds to head home. The dock ring is always open; the ones at Hanging Root Wood, the Fallen Star Workshop and the Bell Court open as the story moves on.

### Getting there
- Find the official NPC **Jeff** at base and accept "Coordinates Above the Clouds" from his usual quest screen. In Ground Zero, beat the chief guarding the Lost Navigation Instrument, take the instrument from its loot box and hand it to Jeff back at base. The dock then offers "Depart for Sky Islands · Qinglan".
- After unlocking it, walk over to the boat; the option only appears there.

### Main line and endings
- On the isles the main line is three official quests, "Light Both Beacons", "The Bell Court Standoff" and "The Homecoming Bell", all accepted and handed in on the islands. No trips back to base.
- Repair the wind vane at Hanging Root Wood and the star lamp at the Fallen Star Workshop; once both beacons burn, head to the Bell Court, settle the last obstacle and ring the Homecoming Bell yourself.
- Zheling and the Silent Bell Keeper can both be reconciled with, or fought. How you settle with them, whether you beat the Windeater, whether the telescope was calibrated and whether the planting record went home all change what the crew write in the Homecoming ship's roster.

### Six residents
- **Fuzhou** (Cloudrise Dock): dock refit, paid repairs for the gear you carry.
- **Miantai** (Hanging Root Wood): moss remedy, priced by how much health you are missing.
- **Weibai** (Windchime Market): lane contracts and the archipelago journal.
- **Qinghe** (Green Terraces): once her planting record is back, a free homecoming meal every raid.
- **Zheling** and the **Silent Bell Keeper**: story branches, peace or a fight.
- All six residents support daily chat, gifts, affinity and marriage. Conversations use the official dialogue box, and residents with a service let you get to business after two lines.

### Chiefs, island lords and the Windeater
- **11 chiefs and island lords**: three island lords (Starforge Foreman, Hanging-Root Huntmaster, Grain Sickle) and eight chiefs (Overlook Stargazer, The Waylayer, Rain Listener, Gnat Piper, Mirror Guest, and Galebreaker Ranger Chaser / Stalker / Warden). The Gnat Piper and the Mirror Guest only come out at night. Each has its own moves, telegraphs and a Boss health bar, and breaking a piece of its gear weakens the matching move.
- Island lords always drop one piece of the gear they wear; chiefs have a chance to drop theirs.
- Once both beacons burn you can face the **Windeater** on Windsong Boardwalk: four phases, the glowing ground ring is the real blast radius, and rock walls block it. The story can be finished without it, but beating it makes the Bell Keeper far easier to talk down. After the ending you can call its echo once per raid.
- Ordinary lane enemies return every raid, so the archipelago never turns into an empty island; every island also has patrols at fixed posts that get tougher the deeper you go.
- Island enemies carry weapons with a quality floor: at least quality 3 for ordinary enemies and quality 5 for named opponents, chiefs and island lords, and the weapon drops into their loot box. Every gun comes with enough spare ammo, so they no longer empty one magazine and just shout that they are out.
- Island enemies have 1.5 times the health and damage of the original boss they are based on.

### Exploring and collecting
- **39 scavenging points** in three tiers (Household Stores, Voyage Supplies, Starworks Cache), each with its own crate look. Contents stay fixed within a raid and refresh on the next one.
- **30 gathering points**: the materials become wind lanterns, windward incense, charms, bentos, salves and other island gear at Fuzhou's dock workbench, Qinghe's stove and Miantai's mortar. Places you repair grow back richer.
- **The isles' lights**: besides the three hearth fires, seven devices are each missing a windcrystal lamp. Light all ten and the night wind stops blowing on the islands.
- **Night wind**: at night (game time 22:00 to 06:00) and on the bridges you slowly build up chill; a full bar gives you a windchill that slows stamina recovery and makes you hungry faster, but never costs health. Wind lanterns, windward incense and the Qinglan Charm each block one layer.
- **Cloud gnats at night**: they arrive in swarms, bite for 1 health, can never kill you, and dodge long-range shots. There are seven counters, and returning frogspawn to Frogsong Pool brings the frogs' song back.
- **Pigeon letters**: one carrier pigeon per raid brings a letter that could not be sent the year of the storm, 12 in all.
- **Hidden-isle puzzles**: the evidence in the four hidden isles is a three-step puzzle, with no penalty for wrong answers.
- **Lane contracts**: clear threats, recover supplies, patrol areas or swat gnats at night, up to three per raid.
- **Shell lots**: the seashells that chiefs and island lords drop can be spent on lots beside the charts at the dock and in Windchime Market: 3 for a small lot, 10 for a big one, and the prize goes straight into your pack.
- **The archipelago journal**: twenty discoveries, letters, the crew roster, the lights and your trip progress. Discoveries also go into the official notes index, readable back at base.

### Exclusive items (36)
- 5 exclusive items: Qinglan Homecoming Badge, Windeater Core, Wind-Vane Compass, Homecoming Bento, Starmoss Salve.
- 13 materials and consumables, including the Cloudmoss Veil, Windcrystal Gnat Zapper and Remedy-Smoke Fan made specifically for cloud gnats.
- 17 pieces of chief and island lord gear. Any two pieces of the Starworks, rootweave hunting, straw-cloak farm or Galebreaker sets give an island-only bonus, and four single pieces (old mailbag, rainhush earmuffs, mossgauze mask, mirrorgrain plate) have their own. They drop on death and never appear in any random reward pool.
- The prologue quest item, the Lost Navigation Instrument.
- Every one of them has a use on the islands; none of them exist just to be sold.

### Music
- The Sky Islands now have their own looping background music.

## New: Fate Echo

- A new mode: a nine-wave challenge that **remembers how you fight**. No need to strip your gear; bring the weapons and supplies you know.
- Entry takes a BossRush ticket and a **Fate Echo Relic** (20,000 at the base vending machine). After picking a map you choose a fate contract, and items are only spent once you confirm; "Not Now" refunds the ticket. Winning wave 9 returns one relic.
- Nine waves in three acts, with your Nemesis on waves 3, 6 and 9. Lose, and the Boss that beat you may come back as next run's Nemesis, one rank stronger.
- **Three counter axes**: range echo, ammo mark and attribute lock. How you fought the last wave decides what the next one throws at you, and the HUD previews the next counter during the break.
- When a multi-Boss wave is down to its last Boss, a 12-second Last Stand window opens.
- Cracking counters and landing Last Stands earns **Resolve** (up to 11); winning wave 9 pays 6 to 10 quality 5-8 Boss rewards depending on your Resolve.
- Press K in a run to abandon it (the ticket and relic are not refunded).
- No mutators, no random events, no cubs.

## New: Black Market Duck Cup

- A new mode where you bet instead of fight. The boat at the base dock now offers "Black Market Duck Cup": one BossRush ticket, no mode item, no need to strip your gear.
- **Team brawls**: every match, a Blue Team on the left and a Red Team on the right storm the ring together, with total power never more than 500 apart. Any BossRush boss can be drawn and official bosses can repeat; the three mod bosses join too (Dragon Descendant 1500, Phantom Witch 500, Skyburner Dragon Lord 2000 power), at most one of each per team.
- **Six matches per season**, each bigger than the last: Blue has 3 to 20 bosses, Red up to 30. Each match allows 3 "Redraw"s, which reroll both teams.
- **Back a side**: pick "Blue Team xN" or "Red Team xN" on the pre-match screen. Odds run x1 to x5, set by the power gap between the teams, and longer odds pay back more.
- **Betting**: bet money from your account (none / 1,000 / 5,000 / 20,000) or items from your backpack. Lose and the house keeps your stake; win and you keep what you staked and get a prize on top. Quitting after the fight starts settles the bet as a loss. In the long run the house always wins, and you can play a whole season without betting.
- **The bell**: your one action per match, bringing a random disaster that hits both teams: Meteor shower, Missile strike, Haste, Frenzy, Rejuvenation or Frost.
- **Spectating**: A / D, ← / → or the mouse buttons switch who you watch, W / ↑ jumps to the Blue Team, S / ↓ to the Red Team; health bars carry "- Blue Team" or "- Red Team". Bosses never hurt their own teammates.
- A match lasts up to 240 seconds; if nobody has won by then, the standing fighters' power × remaining health decides it.
- The ring lands in a random spot each time you enter, drawn from the map's spawn points and checked beforehand for space and a walkable path; twisty maps like the Maze work too.
- After six matches the season enters the **Hall of Fame**, ranked by winning bets and then net winnings: kept across seasons, only 32 seats.
- Kills here do not count for the Duckov Codex, give no experience and do not count toward The Duckov Daily's stats and bounties; no mutators, no random events, no cubs.

## New: Duck King Campaign

- The mod's first story line, six chapters long. A new story, "A Name in the Ledger": the arena's ledger has no entry for the bunker, Jeff wants it written in, and the bookkeeper wants six lines of record. The six chapters are those six lines.
- The quests come from **Jeff**, the game's own NPC at your base, and sit on his quest page next to his vanilla quests. Accepting, checking objectives and handing in all use the game's quest screen; there is no board to build at base.
- The chapters send you to Standard BossRush, From Scratch, Faction War, Blood Hunt and Zombie Mode, and the finale has you hold the sign-up stone in the arena to face the Shadow of the Champion. Chapters 2 and 3 also each have one objective at base: build the garden, and put one Boss trophy on display.
- In-run objectives track themselves, one per line in the tracker at the top right of the screen.
- The six chapters pay 480,000 in total, each files a clue into the game's own notes, and the first three each unlock one Arena Backyard facility.

## New: Jeff's Starter Quests

- Jeff also offers **14 one-time starter quests** that walk you through the new content: Fate Echo, the Black Market Duck Cup, the PetNest, random events, Sky Islands gear, From Scratch, Faction War, Blood Hunt, Zombie Mode, the garden, the trophy display, Affix Forging, reforging and The Duckov Daily.
- Only one is offered at a time, and the next appears once you hand it in. The garden and display quests join the queue after campaign chapters 1 and 2 are handed in, without holding up the rest.
- Each quest tells you what to bring and how to prepare; starting a proper run counts, no clear required. When the objective is done, go back to Jeff at base and press "Complete Quest".
- They pay no extra cash and do not affect the campaign order. Cubs you already have, facilities you already built and qualifying gear you already own all count.

## New: Arena Backyard

- Three base facilities, opened one by one by the first three campaign chapters, all built from the game's own buildings.
- **Garden** (chapter 1): opens the official garden site that has been fenced off at base; pay Shovel ×1 and Poop ×9 to build it. You get 2 of each seed when the garden opens, the base vendor sells more, and each of the three mod Bosses has a roughly 25% chance to drop an extra one. A plant ripens in about 20 minutes and only grows once watered.
- **Harvests transform you**: Dragonbreath Fruit, Emberheart Chili and Umbral Mushroom can be eaten at base or in a raid, turning you into the Dragon Descendant, the Skyburner Dragon Lord or the Phantom Witch for 30 seconds with their attack abilities. Your health and gear stay your own.
- **Display bonus** (chapter 2): put mod Boss trophies on the vanilla weapon display rack or a dummy. Each one adds max health by quality, up to 8 pieces; eight quality-8 pieces add about +21% in total.
- **Jukebox** (chapter 3): the base jukebox gains the mod tracks "Dragon Elegy" and "Umbral Corridors", plus the Sky Islands theme "Mermaid Bay" (Roco Kingdom).

## New: PetNest

- Killing a Boss has a small chance to drop a **relic egg** and always yields **relic souls**; bank 240 souls of one bloodline to condense an egg of exactly that Boss.
- Build a PetNest at base to hatch eggs. Hatching locks in two talents, a temperament and the look, with no rerolls:
  - **Chroma**, about 10%: two of ten colours in order, 90 combinations, with matching particle effects.
  - **Shiny**, about 0.4%: a golden halo and star sparks, purely for collectors.
  - **Pity**: after 9 hatches without a chroma the 10th is guaranteed one; after 99 without a shiny the 100th is guaranteed.
- You can bring one cub per run. It fights with you and adds slots to the official pet backpack; while you are on the field it cannot die, only withdraw wounded with a battle scar.
- Cubs level up to 10 and grow bigger as they do; cubs of the Dragon Descendant, Dragon Lord and Phantom Witch bloodlines wear their Boss's gear.
- The **disaster expedition** is the only place you can truly lose a cub: three destinations, three risk tiers (10 / 30 / 60 real-time minutes), and the death rate is printed on the button before you commit.
- The nest page is a list plus details: click a cub card to select it, and deploying, renaming and releasing follow the selected cub. **Batch release** is supported.
- The Relic Museum tracks the bloodline index, taming achievements and a memorial for cubs lost on expeditions.

## New: Duckov Codex

- A self-keeping Boss collection book: 4,000 at the base shop, used from your backpack, never consumed.
- It records from the moment the mod is installed, book or no book; buying the book just gives you a way to read it.
- Covers every official Boss, official elites, the three custom Bosses and the five zombie Bosses, with total kills, fastest kill, first sighting date and scene.
- Almost every mode counts, vanilla raid maps included. Only finishing blows you land yourself count, and the Black Market Duck Cup never counts.
- Milestone rewards: 50,000 for the first entry, 150,000 for 10, 400,000 for 20 and 1,000,000 for the full catalogue, plus 200,000 for killing any Boss within 10 seconds.

## New: Affix Forging

- Talk to Dingdang and choose "Affix Forging" to spend **affix forge stones** and gold on random affixes for your gear.
- Reforging changes numbers; affixes change **behaviour**: explode on kill, leech on hit, reflect damage from armor. Both can sit on the same item.
- Twelve affixes across common, rare and cursed tiers, each with three strength levels. The cursed tier is strong, and the price is spelled out.
- Works on guns, melee weapons, armor, helmets and masks. Quality decides how many affixes a piece can hold (1 to 3), and you can lock the ones you like before rerolling the rest.
- Forge stones drop from Bosses (about 8%) and are sold in Dingdang's shop from affinity level 10.

## New: Random Events "Duck Life Is Uncertain"

- Something can suddenly happen mid-run. There are **11 events**: Airdrop Supply, Golden Duck Rain, Boss Intrusion, Blood Moon, Wandering Merchant, Feint, Duck King's Fireworks, Duck Parade, and three that hit both sides: Wild Chase, Melee Carnival and Heavy Steps.
- Every event has a banner and a time limit, only one runs at a time, and the first 90 seconds of a run stay quiet.
- They only fire in Standard BossRush, Infinite Hell and From Scratch.
- The Boss from "Boss Intrusion" is not part of the current wave, so you can ignore it and still progress.
- On by default with a low / medium / high frequency tier, and you can switch it off in the mod settings.

## New: The Duckov Daily

- Build a **mailbox** at base (500) and a new issue arrives every in-game day (about 24 real minutes), writing up what you did yesterday.
- Each issue has a front-page story, yesterday's numbers, a daily bounty, weather and gossip, and a check-in wall.
- One bounty per day from five types and three difficulties; completing it is announced in the next issue and paid automatically.
- Check-in runs 30 slots per issue with a small gift every day and higher-quality prizes on milestone slots; from issue 2 on, slots 7 / 14 / 21 / 28 each pay a quality-8 prize.
- Every prize goes to the courier's pending list.

## Now Obtainable: Five New Weapons and the Frost / Thunder Sets

- The 9 items added in v2.2.0 that had no way to get them now do, and **vanilla raid maps count too**:
  - Viper Dagger, Summoning Staff, Energy Shield, Frost Spear and Thunder Ring: 20% drops from the Warden, Big Xing, Goofy Goose, Big Ice and Triple-Shot Man respectively, or a limited buy in Dingdang's shop at affinity level 5.
  - The Frost Set (Frost Crown, Ice Armor) drops from "???" and the Thunder Set (Thunder Horn, Thunder Armor) from the Storm Zone Bosses, 20% per piece; both are also a limited buy in Dingdang's shop at affinity level 6.
- Both sets' 2-piece bonuses were reworked: 50% of incoming ice / electric damage heals you, basic attacks add "Frostbite" / "Thunderbite", and taking hits up close counters the attacker.
- The five weapons got proper grips, trails and hit effects, so if you see the effect the mechanic fired; the two sets had their model size and fit adjusted.
- See each equipment entry for the exact numbers.

## Improved: Music and Sound

- Boss fights now have looping battle music and a victory stinger.
- The Sky Islands have their own looping background music (see above).
- The jukebox gains the Sky Islands theme "Mermaid Bay", and mod tracks are louder overall.

## Improved: Interface and Wiki

- Every mod interface uses a shared skin with a proper finish: buttons play the game's own hover and click sounds and bounce when pressed, and panels and cards get outlines, shadows and a top highlight. Each screen keeps a single primary button, and irreversible actions always ask for confirmation first.
- Text colour is chosen by the actual contrast against its background, so dark panels read more easily.
- Story dialogue uses the official dialogue box, one sentence per screen for long text; campaign clues and Sky Islands discoveries go into the game's own notes, readable back at base.
- The in-game wiki (the adventurer's log) has a new archive layout, new entries for the Sky Islands and every new system, and has been checked line by line against the current game.
- The online wiki has a new layout with Chinese search, click-to-enlarge images and an RSS feed.
- Mod item icons and textures are sharper, with no more blur or blocky noise.
- Player-facing text has been trimmed across the board, so everyday errands take fewer lines.

## Improved: Existing Content

- **Zombie Mode**:
  - Set your own rest time between waves: press "Edit" at the bottom of the reward screen and pick 15 to 300 seconds in 15-second steps. The extraction preparation after a Boss wave uses the same length.
  - New one-use item, the **Portable Safe-Zone Device**, offered among rewards: used in combat it moves the safe zone to you; used during preparation it adds a second safe zone without a merchant.
  - The supply terminal can now **recycle junk**, preferring low-quality ordinary scrap; weapons, ammo, medicine, food, keys and the like are never taken.
- **Awen's Sweep Loot**: when you open the next sweep, anything still left in the old pickup crate is mailed to the courier in one batch with a single summary message; things you put in yourself go back to your backpack.
- **Dragon Cannon**: every ammo type loses its white glow.
- Dragon-line drop gear and the Dragon Cannon are worth more (sell price only; shop prices are unchanged).

## Changed: New Systems Are On by Default

- The PetNest, The Duckov Daily, the Duckov Codex, Affix Forging, random events, the Duck King Campaign, the Arena Backyard and the Black Market Duck Cup are all on by default; there is nothing to enable in the settings.
- Only the "Duck Life Is Uncertain" random events can be switched off in the mod settings. The other systems have no master toggle, just tuning options such as the random event frequency tier.

## Fixes

- Fixed mod-spawned enemies being silently switched off by the game's distance sleep once you moved far away, which left waves that could never finish. This was the single biggest cause of stuck waves.
- Fixed From Scratch Bosses being miscounted by the standard arena's wave logic, which could spawn standard arena Bosses into From Scratch and, in long runs, play the standard clear sequence.
- Fixed wrong faction assignment and incomplete cleanup for enemies spawned in From Scratch.
- Fixed Faction War's roaming merchant failing to initialize.
- Fixed Blood Hunt going a long time without replacing Bosses killed during the opening preparation phase; every phase now replaces them one by one. Also fixed movement under the bloodfire state and added a kill reward prompt.
- Fixed the mutator panel drawing over the backpack and other interfaces.
- Fixed the Dragon Descendant throwing fireworks instead of grenades; it now throws the official incendiary grenade, which leaves a fire zone where it lands.
- Fixed some interface symbols (checks, crosses, minus signs, bullets) showing up in game as blank boxes.
- Fixed some mod text not switching when you change the game language.
- Fixed the Zombie Tide Invitation sometimes not being refunded when Zombie Mode failed to load.
- Fixed the Dragon King Crown (+15% headshot damage) and the Crimson Dragon Helm (+10% headshot damage) showing their headshot bonus in the stat panel without actually applying it.
