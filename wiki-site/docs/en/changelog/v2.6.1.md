# v2.6.1

## Release date
- 2026-11-20
- Fix patch: after quitting and coming back, mod weapons could vanish from your character and mod items in storage could turn into question marks.

## Fixes

- **Weapon slots**: on the first load after starting the game, a mod weapon in a character weapon slot sometimes failed to fit, and saving after that lost it. These weapons are now registered before the save loads, so the main weapon, its attachments and its durability come back as they were.
- **Question marks in storage**: when only part of a mod item's resources had loaded, the game used to treat them as ready, and those items showed up as question marks in storage. Each item is now checked one by one and anything missing is loaded on demand.
- **Held models**: the Inferno Emperor's Realm-Breaking Halberd, Frostmourne, Frost Spear, Summoning Staff, Viper Dagger and totems such as the Residual Sun Shield Shard keep their models in separate bundles, so after loading a save you might not see the model in hand or on your body. Loading a save now fills them in.

::: tip
This version does not change the save format or any item IDs; just update over the old version.
:::
