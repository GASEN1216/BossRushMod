# Residual Sun Shield Shard

Residual Sun Shield Shard is a totem built around dodge timing. Dodge toward an incoming attack, catch one hit inside the brief guard window, then return the absorbed damage with your next direct hit.

## Obtaining and Equipping

- **Source**: follow Jeff's introduction to Experimental Area 37, or defeat Eclipse Bloom in a supported Boss-pool mode. Every victory drops 1 shard.
- **Type**: totem-slot equipment; rarity 6; weight 0.3.
- **Death drops**: protected from death drops while equipped in its matching slot. Keeping it in your backpack does not grant this protection.

## Residual Guard

Starting a successful dodge opens a **0.65-second** shield facing your **dodge direction**. It catches one direct enemy attack within a frontal **120-degree** arc, reducing damage by **70% before health loss**, up to **60 damage blocked**. Cooldown: **4 seconds**.

The direction is fixed when the dodge begins. Side or rear attacks, damage over time, equipment effects, explosions and friendly attacks cannot trigger the block. The guard catches one eligible hit, so a full combo can still get through.

## Afterglow Riposte

A successful block grants a **6-second** charge. Your next direct hit against an enemy deals bonus damage equal to the amount actually blocked, capped at **60**, then consumes the charge. Only one charge can be held at a time.

Damage over time, equipment effects, explosions and friendly hits cannot trigger the riposte. Unequipping, death or changing scenes cancels the shield and charge. Re-equipping does not reset the cooldown. The effect is inactive in the base.

## Combat Rhythm

Watch an enemy wind up, dodge toward the incoming attack, catch it inside the short guard window, then land a reliable direct hit to spend the afterglow. Reposition when enemies surround you; both the shield's direction and its duration are limited.

::: tip
Against an eligible 80-damage frontal hit, 70% absorption blocks 56 damage and leaves 24 damage, preparing a 56-damage riposte. Heavier hits remain subject to the 60-damage block cap.
:::
