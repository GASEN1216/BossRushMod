# Summoning Staff

## What Is It?

A **summoner melee staff** with modest personal damage, but its right-click drops 3 soul warriors straight into the enemy pack **6m ahead of your aim**. They soak fire, and when they fade or fall they detonate on the spot. The skill is in where and when you throw them, not in pressing right-click for three more bodies.

- **Type**: Melee / **Quality**: 5
- **Drop**: 20% from Big Xing (works on vanilla maps too) / Dingdang's Shop (Affinity 5, stock 1)
- Protected from death drops while equipped in its slot; not protected merely by being in the backpack

## Stats

- Dmg 18 / Speed 1.2 / Range 1.8m / Crit 4% / Crit Dmg 1.3x / Pierce 1 / Hit Delay 0.1s
- Block 0.3 / Stamina 6/hit / Bleed 0% / Move Speed 105%

## Soul Projection (Right Click)

- **Cooldown**: 12s / **Cast cost**: 12 stamina (separate from 6 per melee swing)
- Summons **3 soul warriors** **6m ahead of your aim**, spread 2.2m around the drop point
- A wall in the way shortens the throw automatically; if no ground is found the drop falls back to your feet
- Each has **80 HP**, fights on your side, pursues enemies
- Lasts **12 seconds**, then fades (or dies from damage)
- Inactive if you already have 3 active summons; wait for them to expire first

## Soul Burst (Passive)

- Every soul warrior detonates when it **fades or falls**
- **45 ghost damage** within **3m**
- **Never harms you or your allies**
- All three bursting on the same pack adds up to 135

## Staff or Frostmourne?

Both summon, but they are opposite tools:

- **Frostmourne** (Quality 6): **5 permanent undead** hugging you, no timer, they fight until they die. Good for brawling and bodyguard duty
- **Summoning Staff** (Quality 5): **3 short-lived souls** thrown downrange, gone 12 s later in a burst of explosions. Good for placing damage at range

## What It Looks Like

Swings leave a **soul-purple trail**. Casting lights a rune ring under the caster, and each of the three soul warriors gets its own small ring at its landing spot, over a summoning tone that resolves into a solid thud. When a warrior fades or drops, a larger purple ring blows open where it stood. That ring is your confirmation that the burst resolved.

## Tips

- Personal damage stats are intentionally low (18 dmg, 4% crit); you're not the carry
- A good drop point means the summons soak fire *and* all detonate together 12 s later
- 12s cooldown against a 12s lifetime gives a clean loop: throw → soak → burst → throw again
- Against stationary bosses: drop them right on top of the boss, all three bursts land
- Against mobile enemies: drop them on the choke point you want covered and treat the bursts as mines

::: tip
The summons look like zombies but fight for you. They burst when killed too, so throwing them into the thickest pack pays better than parking them somewhere safe.
:::
