# Soulreaper's Requiem

## What Is It?

The Phantom Witch's scythe. Ghost-element melee with **50% curse chance on every hit** and a right-click Curse Realm. All about mobility and crowd control.

## Stats

- **Type**: Melee / **Quality**: 6 / **Element**: Ghost / **Drop**: Phantom Witch (50%) / Protected from death drops while equipped in its slot; not protected merely by being in the backpack
- Dmg 35.5 / Speed 1.56 / Range 2.22m / Crit 5.5% / Crit Dmg 1.33x / Pierce 3.8
- Bleed 8% / Block 0.45 / Stamina 6.5/hit / Hit Delay 0.09s / **Move Speed 120%** / Gem Slots 2

## Soulreaving Power (Passive)

- All attacks deal Ghost damage
- **50% chance** to apply Curse: -30% walk and run speed per stack, max 3 stacks, 5s duration
- 3 stacks = **-90% speed** (summed with the target's other percentage speed modifiers), but never a root

## Requiem Realm (Right Click)

- **Cooldown**: 12s / **Stamina**: 20 / **Windup**: 0.25s
- Violet rune circle, 4m radius, 4s duration
- 22 Ghost damage every 0.5s + Curse to everything inside

## Tips

- **120% move speed** = best kiting weapon in the mod
- Curse stacking = free crowd control. Keep the slow applied while repositioning
- The Realm is great for holding chokepoints: stand in it and let the ticks do the work
- Raw damage slightly below Frostmourne, but mobility + CC more than compensate

::: tip
Core strength = mobility and control, not raw numbers.
:::
