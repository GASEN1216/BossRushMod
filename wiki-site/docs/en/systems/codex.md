# Duck King Codex

# What is it

- A Boss collection book that keeps its own ledger: every Boss you personally kill lights up its entry.
- Each entry records total kills, the date you first met it, which mode the first kill happened in,
  and your fastest clear time.
- Locked entries show as a black silhouette; the full-color portrait appears once you've killed it.
- **Every mode counts**: Standard BossRush, Infinite Hell, From Scratch, Faction War, Blood Hunt,
  Fate Echo, the Duck Cup, Zombie Apocalypse — even Bosses you happen to kill on vanilla raid maps.

# How to open it

- Buy a `Duck King Codex` from the base shop and use it from your inventory to open the panel.
  **Using it does not consume it** — buy once and you're set.
- The Wiki book also has a shortcut that jumps straight to the codex panel.

# What's in the panel

- A progress bar at the top with milestone marks, so you can see how far the next reward is.
- A card grid below: portrait, name, kill count, fastest kill, first-seen date.
- Click any card to enlarge the portrait.

# Milestone rewards

Hitting certain unlock counts fires achievements and pays out cash through the achievement system
you already know:

- Unlock your 1st entry
- Unlock 10
- Unlock 20
- **Full collection** (the biggest payout)
- Plus a novelty one: kill any Boss within 10 seconds

# Questions you might have

- **Do kills by others count?** No. Only kills where you dealt the fatal blow. Companions, pets,
  mercenaries and environmental damage don't count.
- **Does my PetNest companion dying get recorded?** No, companions are explicitly excluded.
- **How is "fastest kill" measured?** From the moment you first damage it until it drops.
  Hiding to stall will inflate that number.
- **What happens across save slots?** The codex follows the **save slot**, so each save collects
  separately. That keeps milestone payouts consistent with the slot's own assets.
- **I disabled a Boss in the Boss Filter — do I lose its record?** No. Entries you've already
  collected stay in the codex.

# Toggle

The whole codex can be turned off in ModConfig (on by default). Turning it off stops recording;
existing data is kept.
