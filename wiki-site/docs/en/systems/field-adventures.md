# Field Adventures

Ordinary raids now offer three optional diversions: grow a hungry mimic, deliver Awen's temperamental parcels, and test Dingdang's oversized weapon rigs. They appear on eligible ordinary raid maps, outside the base and BossRush combat modes. An activity may not appear if no suitable open ground can be found nearby.

## Gluttonous Mimic

A little after entering a map, a giant rumbling chest may appear nearby. It starts passive. Approach to feed it or choose "Feast" to start fighting. Shooting it also starts combat immediately.

Before each meal, a confirmation shows the exact item, amount and body part it will grow. Nothing is consumed until you confirm. It accepts up to three meals, and the third starts combat automatically.

- **12 rounds of ammunition**: grows four cannons and learns a fan volley shown by warning lines on the ground.
- **1 grenade**: grows bomb sacs and learns to lob giant bombs into marked circles.
- **1 medicine item**: grows a huge bottle and learns a charged heal. Deal a total of 45 damage during the charge to interrupt it.

Each meal makes the whole chest larger and adds 240 maximum health. It starts with 600 health and reaches 1320 after three meals. Repeating a food type strengthens its matching attack. You may start fighting after one or two meals, or feed it nothing at all.

It only accepts ordinary quality 1-3 items in your backpack's outer inventory. Locked slots, wishlisted items, quest items, custom items, containers and partially used medicine are excluded. Ammo needs a single stack of at least 12 rounds, grenades must deal explosive damage, and medicine must restore health. The lowest-quality eligible item is selected first, then the cheapest within that quality.

Defeat it to make it spit cash and **twice the amount of each ingredient you fed it** onto the ground. Cash starts at 600, plus 400 per meal, up to 1800. Pick up the drops. At most one mimic appears per raid; moving between areas of the same raid does not refresh that allowance.

::: warning
Feeding consumes the item immediately. The third meal automatically starts combat. Leaving the current area, dying or ending the raid without defeating the mimic does not refund its meals.
:::

## Awen's Odd-Parcel Delivery

Find Awen during a raid and switch his interaction group to "Odd-parcel delivery". If he is absent nearby, a winged dispatch box appears near your entry point, marked as "Awen's odd-parcel dispatch" on the map.

Read the invoice and choose a parcel. Declining costs nothing. The parcel appears beside you; interact to pick it up, then carry it to the map's "Odd-parcel receipt desk". The desk is normally placed at a reachable location 28-45 metres from where you accepted the order.

- **Quacking Egg**: a giant duck egg with a beak and wings. It periodically quacks while you carry it around, attracting nearby enemies. Sprinting agitates it faster. At full pressure it bounces, loses 16 integrity and tries to land in a nearby clear spot. Standing still calms it; setting it down calms it faster.
- **Angry Soda**: an oversized can with foam and a bent straw. Pressure builds while carried and rises faster when you sprint. Set it down to vent. At full pressure it erupts into a foam fountain, loses 22 integrity and tries to bounce into a clear spot.

The parcel's light strip shows pressure. Its interaction label shows pressure and integrity. Interact again to pick up or set down; switch to "Parcel details / cancel" to check the distance and expected payout or cancel the order. The order ends if integrity reaches zero.

Carry the parcel close to the receipt desk and choose "Deliver odd parcel". The enormous stamp comes down, celebration particles fly, and cash is paid directly. Payment is **1200 plus 12 times the remaining integrity**, reaching 2400 for an undamaged parcel. If payment is temporarily unavailable, interact again to retry.

You may accept two orders per raid. Cancelled orders, ruined parcels and leaving the delivery area still use their accepted slots. Moving between areas of the same raid does not reset the allowance. Parcels are supplied by the activity; they take no backpack space and do not replace your equipment.

::: tip
The egg's calls can serve as a temporary lure. Stop and let it settle when you need quiet. Plan resting spots for soda, set it down to vent, then carry on.
:::

## Dingdang's Dangerous Prototypes

Find Dingdang on an ordinary raid map and choose "Dangerous prototypes". A prototype bench also appears near your entry point for borrowing, checking progress and returning rigs. Hold the gun you want to test, choose a rig and confirm installation.

The prototype attaches to the gun held when you borrowed it. Once its pressure lights fill, **fire normally** to release an extra experimental attack. Your original gun still fires and consumes ammunition as usual.

- **Duckback Mk.I**: a huge cannon sleeve charges every 2.5 seconds. The next shot sends out a thick blast and pushes you backwards a short distance. Land 6 actual blast hits within 150 seconds to pass.
- **Ricochet Sun**: a glowing sun rig charges every 1.6 seconds. The next shot fires a beam that can bounce off walls up to 3 times. Direct hits are weak; bounced hits are much stronger. Land 3 bounced hits within 150 seconds to pass.

Switching guns hides the rig. Switch back to the original gun to continue, while the timer keeps running. You may borrow again after timing out or returning an unfinished test. Death or ending the raid recalls the rig; it is not permanent equipment you can take home.

After passing, return to Dingdang or the bench and choose "Return and claim reward". Each prototype grants **1 Cold Quench Fluid per raid**, for 2 bottles if you complete both. If your bag is full, the reward is placed beside the return point. A failed delivery can be retried. Once you claim a prototype's reward, that same test is closed for the rest of the raid.

::: tip
Ricochet Sun requires actual hits after a wall bounce. Aim at a wall near the enemy and fold the beam toward them; repeatedly shooting straight at the enemy does not advance this test.
:::
