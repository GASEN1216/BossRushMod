# Random Events: "Duck Life Is Uncertain"

# What is it

- Mid-fight, things just happen: a supply crate slams down, a blood moon rises, money rains from
  the sky, or a Boss that has no business being here walks straight in.
- Every event has a banner announcement, a fixed duration, and cleans itself up when it ends.
- How it differs from Mutators: mutators are rolled at the start and stay fixed all run;
  events fire mid-run and are time-limited.

# What can happen

| Event | Roughly what it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Airdrop Supply** | A supply crate drops in. The landing is loud and pulls nearby enemies over to fight you for it |
| **Blood Moon** | The screen goes red and every enemy on the field gets stronger for a while. Kills during it pay extra cash |
| **Uninvited Guest** | An extra Boss is pulled from the pool and crashes the fight. Killing it pays a bonus; stall long enough and it leaves on its own |
| **Wandering Merchant** | A limited-time stall selling ammo, medical and one random high-tier item, at a markup |
| **Feint** | You hear noises from every direction and enemies wander off to investigate. Nothing is actually there |
| **Duck King's Fireworks** | Pure fireworks. Zero damage. It just looks good |
| **Golden Duck Rain** | It rains money. In Infinite Hell the cash magnet vacuums it up for you |
| **Duck Parade** | A line of ducks waddles across the battlefield and leaves |

# Important: the intruder Boss does not affect waves

The Boss spawned by **Uninvited Guest** is **not counted as part of the current wave**.
You don't need to kill it to advance, and it won't skip a wave by dying.
Fight it or hide until it leaves — your call.

# Which modes trigger events

Only **Standard BossRush, Infinite Hell and From Scratch**.

Faction War, Blood Hunt, Fate Echo, the Duck Cup, Zombie Apocalypse and vanilla raid maps
do **not** trigger events — those modes have their own pacing and rules, and forcing events
in would only fight with them.

# Toggle

ModConfig can disable the whole system (on by default) and set a frequency tier
(low / standard / high), which controls the gap between events and the per-run cap.
Only one event is ever active at a time.
