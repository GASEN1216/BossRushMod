# Systems

A run in the arena is only the beginning. What you get afterwards, how you reshape your gear, how you raise a hatchling, how the story moves on — BossRush keeps a whole second layer of systems back at base. This page groups them by **what you want to do**; the full card index is at the bottom.

## Where the loot comes from

- **Loot & Rewards** — Read this first: crates, cash pools and strict rewards, mode by mode
- **Death Wraith** — Turns one death into a rematch you have to win back
- **Random Events** — Supply drops, blood moons and worse: everything that can happen mid-run
- **Mutator System** — 28 mutators rolled at the start of a run that rewrite its rules

## Reshaping your gear

- **Reforge System** — Reroll a piece's numbers at Dingdang's, lock the affixes you want with quench fluid
- **Affix Forging** — Reforging changes numbers; forging changes behaviour by adding affixes

## Things to build and raise at base

- **StarWish Fountain** — A buildable wishing fountain for your base
- **PetNest** — Pick up eggs from bosses, hatch them at base, take the hatchling to war
- **Arena Backyard** — Farm, display trophies, pick your battle anthem; unlocked chapter by chapter through the campaign
- **The Duckov Daily** — One issue per in-game day, reporting on what you did yesterday
- **Duck King Codex** — A boss collection book that keeps its own records

## Story and relationships

- **Duck King Campaign** — Six story contracts that string every existing mode together
- **Affinity & Marriage** — Ten affinity tiers, gifts and dates, all the way to a wedding

## Tuning the mod

- **Boss Filter** — Ctrl+F10 to ban a boss or tune its weight in Infinite Hell
- **Configuration** — Every tunable knob and how hot-reload works

::: tip
New here? Start with "Loot & Rewards" and the "Mutator System" — they explain what happens when a run ends. Come back to the base systems once your first relic souls and eggs start dropping.
:::
