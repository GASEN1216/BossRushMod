## Affix Forging

### What is it

Reforging changes **numbers** — a few more points of armor, a little more fire rate.
Affix forging changes **behavior**: your gun detonates corpses on kill, your blade drinks
health on every hit, your armor throws damage back at whoever dealt it.

A single piece can carry both reforged stats and affixes. The two systems never touch.

- **12 affixes** across three tiers: Common, Rare and **Cursed**.
- Works on **guns, melee weapons, body armor, helmets and face masks**. Not totems or bags.
- Every Cursed affix is a trade, not a gift — obviously strong, with the price written out.

### How to use it

- Talk to Dingdang and pick `Affix Forging`.
- Select a piece of gear, then spend **1 Affix Forge Stone + money** to roll one affix onto it.
  The money follows the reforge price curve, so Dingdang's affinity discounts apply here too.
- How many affixes a piece can hold depends on its **quality**: **1** at quality 4 or below,
  **2** at quality 5-6, **3** at quality 7+. The slot count is frozen at the first forge and
  never changes afterwards.
- **Lock** an affix you like (costs `2` stones) and a re-roll leaves it alone, rolling only the
  unlocked slots. **Unlocking is free.**
- Affix names show up directly on the item details panel, so you can see at a glance what a
  gun is carrying.

#### The roll is weighted

- Tier: Common `62%`, Rare `30%`, Cursed `8%`.
- Each affix also rolls one of three strengths: T1 `60%`, T2 `30%`, T3 `10%`.

So "a Rare at T3" is about a `3%` outcome, and "a Cursed at T3" is under `1%`. The tables
below list **T1 / T2 / T3** — you will usually get the leftmost number.

### Where stones come from

- **Dingdang's shop** — unlocks at affinity Lv.2, up to 5 per restock. This is the steady line.
- **Boss drops** — around `8%`, so roughly one stone per standard arena run. This is the lucky line.
  Stones land **on the boss itself**, not in the loot crate — search the body, same as relic eggs.
  Covers the three standard difficulties, From Scratch, Faction War and Blood Hunt; Fate Echo, the Black Market Duck Cup and Zombie Mode do not drop them.

Neither line is generous enough to let you re-roll forever. That's deliberate: stones being
tight is what makes "should I lock this one?" an actual question.

### The affix list

Each affix only goes on certain gear types, noted after the name.

#### Common (5)

- **Lifesteal** (weapons) — Hits restore `0.6% / 1.0% / 1.5%` of max health, 0.4s cooldown.
  A shotgun blast counts once, so don't expect a full heal per trigger.
- **Slaughter** (all) — Kills restore `3% / 5% / 8%` of max health. No cooldown, and very
  comfortable sustain while clearing trash.
- **Bulwark** (armor/helmet/mask) — `1 / 2 / 3` armor for 4s after being hit, up to 3 stacks.
  The harder they come, the thicker you get.
- **Swift Hand** (**guns only**) — Reload speed `+15% / +25% / +35%` for 5s after a kill, up to
  2 stacks. On a kill streak you barely wait for reloads. Melee weapons can't roll this one.
- **Thorns** (armor/helmet/mask) — Reflect `10% / 18% / 28%` of the damage taken back at the
  attacker, 0.35s cooldown.

#### Rare (4)

- **Death Burst** (all) — Kills detonate the corpse for `35 / 55 / 80` fire damage in a
  `2.5 / 3.0 / 3.5` metre radius. **It never hurts you**, so feel free to blow it up in your face.
- **Frenzy** (all) — Fire rate and mobility `+4% / +6% / +9%` for 6s after a kill, up to 5
  stacks. Fully stacked that's `+20%` to `+45%` — you get faster the deeper into the pile you go.
- **Hawk Eye** (weapons) — Passive: gun and melee crit chance `+4% / +7% / +11%`. Nothing to
  trigger; just hold it.
- **Overcharge** (weapons) — Hits add an electric strike for `12% / 20% / 30%` of the damage
  dealt, 0.25s cooldown.

#### Cursed (3 - strong, with a price)

All three go on any supported gear, and all three are passive: they apply as long as you're
holding or wearing the piece.

- **Blood Rage** — All weapon damage `+10% / +16% / +24%`, but max health `−12% / −18% / −25%`.
- **Glass Cannon** — All weapon damage `+15% / +24% / +35%`, but armor `−1 / −2 / −3`.
- **Death Pact** — Kills restore `10% / 14% / 20%` of max health, but you lose
  `0.8% / 1.2% / 1.8%` of max health per second. **It can never kill you** — it always leaves
  you at 1 HP, and there's no drain inside the base.

[tip] "All weapon damage" really does mean all of it: Blood Rage on a helmet still boosts the gun in your hands. Putting Cursed affixes on armor is the efficient play — you keep the damage and your weapon slots stay free for something else.

[tip] Cursed affixes work best as a set: stack Blood Rage and Glass Cannon for `+59%` damage, then use Death Pact or Slaughter to keep the health topped up. Commit fully or don't touch them — half-measures just leave you fragile without the payoff.

### Common questions

- **Do affixes only work while I'm holding the item?** Yes. Weapon affixes need that weapon in
  hand; armor affixes need the piece worn. Gear sitting in your stash or backpack does nothing.
- **Does an NPC carrying the same weapon trigger them?** No. Only you.
- **Does reforging wipe affixes?** No. Reforging and affix forging never touch each other -
  one changes stats, the other changes behavior.
- **If I sell an item and buy it back, are the affixes still there?** Yes. Affixes are written
  onto the item itself and travel with it.
- **Do two pieces with the same affix stack?** Yes.
- **Can one item roll the same affix twice?** No. Duplicates are never rolled onto one piece.
- **Can I forge in Zombie Mode?** No. The temporary goblin there doesn't offer it.
- **What if I roll something I don't want?** Lock the ones you're keeping and spend another
  stone to re-roll. Only the unlocked slots change.

### Do I need to enable it?

No. Affix forging is on by default - just talk to the Goblin. There is nothing to switch on.
