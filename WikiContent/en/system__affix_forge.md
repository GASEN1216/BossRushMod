## Affix Forging

### What is it

- Reforging changes numbers; affix forging changes **behavior**: make your gun explode on kill,
  leech health on hit, or make your armor reflect damage back.
- 12 affixes across three tiers: Common, Rare and **Cursed**. Cursed ones are strong but every
  single one comes with a real drawback.
- Works on guns, melee weapons, body armor, helmets and face masks.

### How to use it

- Talk to the Goblin and pick `Affix Forging`.
- Select a piece of gear, then spend `Affix Forge Stones` + money to roll an affix onto it.
- How many affixes a piece can hold depends on its rarity: 1 slot for common gear, 2 for mid,
  3 for high rarity.
- You can **lock** an affix you like (costs extra stones) so a re-roll won't touch it.
  Unlocking is free.
- Affix names show up directly on the item details panel.

### Where stones come from

- The Goblin's shop (affinity-gated)
- The StarWish Fountain reward pool
- Boss drops (low chance)

### The affix list

**Common**

- **Leech** — heal on hit (has an internal cooldown, so don't expect a shotgun to top you off in one shot)
- **Culling** — heal on kill
- **Bulwark** — brief armor boost after taking a hit
- **Swift Hand** — brief reload speed boost after a kill
- **Thorns** — reflect part of the damage you take back at the attacker

**Rare**

- **Volatile** — targets explode where they die
- **Frenzy** — stacking attack and move speed on kills
- **Hawkeye** — permanent crit rate while worn
- **Overcharge** — hits deal an extra burst of electric damage

**Cursed (strong, but they cost you)**

- **Blood Pact** — big damage boost while held, at the cost of max health
- **Glass Cannon** — more damage, less armor
- **Death Pact** — big heal on kill, but you bleed out continuously.
  **It will not kill you** — it always leaves you at 1 HP minimum

### Questions you might have

- **Do affixes only work while I'm holding the gear?** Yes. Weapon affixes only fire while you're
  actually holding that weapon; armor affixes only while worn. Gear sitting in storage or your
  backpack does nothing.
- **Will an NPC holding the same weapon trigger them?** No, only you.
- **Will reforging wipe my affixes?** No. Reforging and affix forging don't touch each other —
  one changes stats, the other changes affixes.
- **If I sell an item and buy it back, are the affixes still there?** Yes. Affixes are stored on the
  item itself and travel with it.
- **Do two pieces with the same affix stack?** Yes.
- **Can I forge in Zombie Apocalypse?** No, the temporary Goblin there doesn't offer it.

### Toggle

Can be disabled in ModConfig (on by default). Disabling hides the interaction and stops affixes
from firing, but **affix data already on your gear is preserved** and comes back when you re-enable it.
