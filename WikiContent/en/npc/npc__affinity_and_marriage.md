## Affinity and Marriage

### Affinity System

10 levels for Dingdang and Yu Zhi. Chat daily, give gifts, unlock services and story content.

#### Level Thresholds

- Lv.1: 0 → Lv.2: 50 → Lv.3: 150 → Lv.4: 300 → Lv.5: 500
- Lv.6: 750 → Lv.7: 1,050 → Lv.8: 1,400 → Lv.9: 1,800 → Lv.10: 2,300

#### How to Gain Affinity

- **Daily chat**: Dingdang +40 / Yu Zhi +30 (once/day)
- **Liked gift**: +80 / **Neutral gift**: +20 / **Disliked**: -40 (Yu Zhi -60 for Brick Stone)
- **Diamond Ring or Crown**: +500 / **Birthday Cake**: +150

#### Gift Notes

Every NPC in the affinity tab has a "Gift notes" section with a Likes line and a Dislikes line. Both start as "not figured out yet", and each gift that hits a preference adds it:

- a specific item the NPC loves or hates is recorded as that item;
- an item from a category the NPC likes (recipes, consumables, medicine and so on) is recorded as that category;
- a collectible whose card says who likes it is recorded as that item when you give it to the right person;
- neutral gifts record nothing.

The first time you learn something, a small paper slip reading "Gift notes updated" sticks to the affinity popup. Entries you haven't found only show how many remain, never what they are, and discovered notes save alongside affinity.

Walk up to Dingdang, Yu Zhi, the courier or the residents of Sky Island and Tidegate and their name floats above their head: it fades in within 8 m and fades out beyond 10 m. Nothing shows from afar, and names hide with the game HUD or when paused.

#### Decay

Skip a day (no chat or gift) = **-15 Affinity** next day. Don't neglect your NPCs.

### Marriage

#### Requirements

- Affinity Lv.10 with the target NPC
- Wedding Chapel built and placed
- Diamond Ring in hand

#### Wedding

- Give the Ring → proposal accepted
- Full-screen cutscene plays (fallback dialogue if animation missing)
- Heart bubble + anniversary date appear
- NPC moves to the Wedding Chapel

#### After Marriage

- **Location**: Wedding Chapel
- **Dialogue**: Marriage-exclusive lines
- **Follow / Home**: Ask your spouse to follow you from the chapel, or send them home
- Follow state persists across scene changes
- If Affinity drops below Lv.10, spouse auto-returns home with a warning
- **Dingdang perk**: Free Cold Quench Fluid during daily chat
- **Yu Zhi perk**: Free Calming Drops during daily chat
- Wedding cutscene can be replayed at the chapel

#### Rules

- **Monogamy** — one spouse at a time
- **Re-proposal**: Refused if already married; ring not consumed
- **Cheating**: Give a ring to someone else while married = ring consumed, that NPC loses 120 Affinity, and your spouse scolds you next chat. From the second time on your spouse loses Affinity too, 40 more each time (-40 the second time, -80 the third...)

#### Divorce

- Talk to spouse at the chapel → select "Divorce"
- Affinity resets to zero
- NPC returns to original location
