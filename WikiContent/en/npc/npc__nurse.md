## Yu Zhi (Nurse)

### Who Is She?

A nurse who came out of J-Lab, with the marks of its experiments still on her legs. After the lab blew up she ran to Duckov and opened a field clinic. **Your healer and debuff cleaner**, with her own Affinity storyline.

### Services

- **Chat** (from start) — Daily chat, Affinity +30/day
- **Gift** (from start) — 1 gift/day
- **Healing** (from start) — Restores full HP + removes treatable debuffs. Cost drops with Affinity

### Affinity Rewards

- **Lv.2** — 10% healing discount
- **Lv.3** — Free Calming Drops ×5
- **Lv.4** — 20% discount
- **Lv.5** — Story: Yu Zhi's Memories
- **Lv.6** — 25% discount
- **Lv.7** — 30% discount
- **Lv.8** — Peace Charm; heart bubble during chat
- **Lv.9** — 40% discount
- **Lv.10** — Story: Yu Zhi's Confession

### Gifts

- **Likes**: Diamond and consumable/medical items (+80); Birthday Cake (+150)
- **Loves**: Diamond Ring and Crown (+500)
- **Neutral**: Other items (+20)
- **Dislikes**: Brick Stone, which reminds her of the lab (-60)

### Marriage

Affinity Lv.10 + Diamond Ring = wedding. She moves to the Wedding Chapel with marriage-exclusive dialogue.

See Affinity and Marriage for details.

### Tips

- 40% healing discount at max Affinity — **worth the investment**
- Calming Drops and nurse healing remove treatable debuffs such as bleeding, fractures and poison, but not the Phantom Witch's **Ghost Curse**; Ghost Curse alone does not allow Calming Drops to be used
- Peace Charm is passive. Keep it in your inventory: when you get hit below half health, it has a 10% chance to heal you to full, at most once per map
