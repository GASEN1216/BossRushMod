## Achievement List

### Overview
This mod currently contains 46 achievements across 9 categories. Completing achievements grants cash rewards. Press L (default, configurable) to open the achievement panel and check your progress.

### Basic Completion
- First Steps: Clear a Full Campaign for the first time (Standard+). Reward `$5,000`, difficulty `★`
- Easy Peasy: Clear a Full Campaign with the A Snap lineup (Standard+). Reward `$10,000`, difficulty `★`
- Getting Serious: Clear a Full Campaign with the Getting Interesting lineup (Standard+). Reward `$25,000`, difficulty `★★`
- Nightmare Cleared: Clear a Full Campaign on the Nightmare challenge. Reward `$800,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`
- From Scratch: Complete 10 waves in From Scratch. Reward `$30,000`, difficulty `★★`
- Hell Wave 10: Survive 10 waves in Infinite Hell (Standard+). Reward `$50,000`, difficulty `★★★`
- Hell Wave 25: Survive 25 waves in Infinite Hell (Standard+). Reward `$150,000`, difficulty `★★★`
- Hell Wave 50: Survive 50 waves in Infinite Hell (Standard+). Reward `$350,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
- Hell Wave 100: Survive 100 waves in Infinite Hell (Standard+). Reward `$800,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`
- Hell Wave 200: Survive 200 waves in Infinite Hell (Standard+). Reward `$2,000,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`

[tip] In the arena's War Writ, picking the Relaxed challenge or a Short or Long March means clear, flawless, speedrun and cumulative clear achievements don't count. On Infinite Hell, the Relaxed challenge doesn't count toward Hell wave achievements.

### Cumulative
- Regular: Clear 10 Full Campaigns in total (Standard+). Reward `$50,000`, difficulty `★★`
- Seasoned Challenger: Clear 50 Full Campaigns in total (Standard+). Reward `$250,000`, difficulty `★★★`
- Legendary Hunter: Clear 100 Full Campaigns in total (Standard+). Reward `$800,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
- Novice Hunter: Defeat 50 bosses in total. Reward `$20,000`, difficulty `★`
- Veteran: Defeat 100 bosses in total. Reward `$80,000`, difficulty `★★`
- Five Hundred Down: Defeat 500 bosses in total. Reward `$500,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
- Immortal Warlord: Defeat 1000 bosses in total. Reward `$1,500,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`

### Flawless
- Flawless A Snap: Clear a Full Campaign with the A Snap lineup without taking damage (Standard+). Reward `$80,000`, difficulty `★★★`
- Flawless Getting Interesting: Clear a Full Campaign with the Getting Interesting lineup without taking damage (Standard+). Reward `$250,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
- Flawless From Scratch: Complete 5 waves in From Scratch without taking damage. Reward `$350,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
- Iron Will: Survive 10 waves in Infinite Hell without taking damage (Standard+). Reward `$500,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`

### Speedrun
- Lightning Run: Within 5 minutes, clear a Full Campaign or reach Infinite Hell wave 10 (Standard+), or clear From Scratch. Reward `$30,000`, difficulty `★★`
- Speed Demon: Within 3 minutes, clear a Full Campaign or reach Infinite Hell wave 10 (Standard+), or clear From Scratch. Reward `$120,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
- Time Assassin: Within 2 minutes, clear a Full Campaign or reach Infinite Hell wave 10 (Standard+), or clear From Scratch. Reward `$400,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`
- Instant Kill (Hidden): Within 1 minute, clear a Full Campaign or reach Infinite Hell wave 10 (Standard+), or clear From Scratch. Reward `$800,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`

[tip] "Instant Kill" is a hidden achievement and will not appear in the achievement panel until unlocked.

### Boss Kill

- Dragonslayer: Defeat the Skyburner Dragon Lord for the first time. Reward `$100,000`, difficulty `★★★`
- Flawless Dragonslayer: Defeat the Skyburner Dragon Lord without taking damage. Reward `$500,000`, difficulty `★★★★★`
- Master Dragonslayer: Defeat the Skyburner Dragon Lord 10 times in total. Reward `$600,000`, difficulty `★★★★`
