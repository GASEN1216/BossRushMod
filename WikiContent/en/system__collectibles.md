## Collectibles & Album

### What they are

Every now and then a crate holds one extra old object: a missing-duck poster, a community-games bronze medal, a chunk of lighthouse lens. These are **collectibles**: 100 in total, grouped into nine map sets, two global sets and a handful of treasures.

You can sell them for their value, log them in the album to complete sets for cash and small permanent bonuses, give them to an NPC who likes them, or put them on display at base.

### Where they come from

- Scene crates on the official raid maps (Ground Zero, Hidden Warehouse, Farm Town, J-Lab, Storm Zone, Test Area 37) and on the three mod maps (Qinglan Archipelago, Tidegate, Last Shift Yard).
- A crate is checked **only the first time you open it**. On a hit it gets **one extra slot**, searched like any other item; rarer pieces take longer to reveal. Reopening the same crate never rolls again.
- **Collectibles have their own pool. They never take a vanilla slot or vanilla odds, so vanilla items are not any harder to find.** They also stay out of the Wishing Fountain, the Daily and every random reward pool.
- Ordinary crates give roughly one in 25; high-value crates (safes, weapon crates, tactical crates, computer cases, J-Lab crates, treasure chests) roughly one in 8. At most 3 per raid.
- Pieces you haven't logged yet turn up more often than ones you already have, so duplicates won't stall a set for long.
- Corpse boxes, tombs, airdrops, reward crates, delivery boxes, storage and the pet backpack never hold them, and none drop during Boss Rush modes, Zombie Mode or the Duck Cup. Last Shift Yard's sealed cabinets, lead-sealed side cabinet and drop crates are stocked per train, so they don't either.
- On the Qinglan Archipelago they top out at tier 6; Tidegate's underwater crates top out at tier 4, so the underwater jackpot works as before.

### Treasures

Six treasures can appear anywhere, though hardly anyone has seen one: the Savings Vault Gold Bar, the Solid Gold Rubber Duck, the Qinglan Star Core, the Duck King's Scepter and Skyburner's Dragon Pearl (tier 7, worth about 340,000 to 760,000), and the **First Duck King's Golden Egg** (tier 8, worth 29,470,423, more than the crown). Tier 8 only comes from high-value crates and is rarer than the crown. Treasures can be logged but don't count toward set rewards.

### Value

Tiers 1 to 6 follow the value range of official collectibles and luxuries of the same tier: about 800 to 1,300 at tier 1, 1,200 to 2,200 at tier 2, 1,900 to 2,800 at tier 3, 3,500 to 6,700 at tier 4, 6,100 to 10,700 at tier 5, and 18,000 to 42,000 at tier 6.

### The album

- Talk to **Jeff** at base and take "Odds and Ends". Jeff hands you a **Collector's Album** when you accept (into storage if your backpack is full), and from then on the base vending machine sells it too.
- Right-click the album in your inventory to open it. Sets are listed on the left and the current set's pieces on the right; unlogged pieces show their icon as a silhouette, but you can still read their name and story. Reading it uses nothing up.
- At base, press **Register** to open an empty crate: put collectibles in and close it to log them. **Logged items are taken**, so hold back anything you would rather sell or give away. Anything already logged, or not a collectible, goes back to your backpack (or to storage if it's full).
- Log one piece, then go back to Jeff to turn in the quest. There's no extra reward; the album is the reward.
- You can browse the album during a raid, but you can only register at base.

### Set rewards

Completing a set pays its cash at once and grants a small permanent bonus (applied every time you enter a scene, never stacked twice):

- Ground Zero: 30,000 cash, max stamina +5
- Hidden Warehouse: 30,000 cash, carry weight +1 kg
- Farm Town: 30,000 cash, food and drink effects +5%
- J-Lab: 30,000 cash, healing received +5%
- Storm Zone: 30,000 cash, run speed +2%
- Test Area 37: 30,000 cash, reload speed +3%
- Qinglan Archipelago: 30,000 cash, stamina recovery +5%
- Tidegate: 30,000 cash, max hydration +5
- Last Shift Yard: 30,000 cash, hearing +5%
- Arena Legends: 150,000 cash, headshot damage +5%
- Companions' Keepsakes: 150,000 cash, backpack slots +1
- All 11 sets complete: another 500,000 cash

### On display

Every collectible counts as an **exhibit**, so it can go in the base **Display Cabinet**, which shows the object itself. The cabinet only shows up in the build menu once you have built the **Bitcoin Miner** and finished "Toy Collector II". A collectible dropped on the ground looks like itself too.

### Gifts

Collectibles make good gifts. Several NPCs have favourites: Dingdang likes the first hammer and the heirloom anvil, Old Zha likes Tidegate's old things, Yu Zhi likes the nurse cap pin, and each Qinglan resident likes the island pieces tied to their own life. Rarer gifts raise affinity more.

[tip] Unsure whether to sell, give or log something? Open the album first to see which set it belongs to and how many pieces that page still needs.
