## Island Chief Gear

[tip] Found on seven Sky Islands chiefs, plus the Galebreaker Warden in Jeff's prelude: the Waylayer, the Rain Listener, the Gnat Piper and the Mirror Guest each wear one piece and leave it behind 30% of the time; the three Galebreaker Rangers each wear one piece of the Galebreaker set and leave it behind 40% of the time. The piece takes one slot in the loot box; the rest of the box is whatever the chief was carrying.

### What Is It?

**The single piece each of seven chiefs wears**: the Old Mailbag comes from the Waylayer at the Upturned Post Hut, the Rainhush Earmuffs from the Rain Listener in the Rainlisten Grotto, the Mossgauze Mask from the Gnat Piper at Frogsong Pool and the Mirrorgrain Plate from the Mirror Guest at Mirrorwater Temple; the Galebreaker Mantle, Galebreaker Pack and Galebreaker Hood make up the Galebreaker set and come from the Galebreaker Ranger (Chaser), (Stalker) and (Warden) holding the three return relay platforms. Worn, they give the game's usual pack capacity, hearing, head armour or body armour; on the isles each also has a use — an extra pigeon letter, longer chief warnings, gnats that stop dodging, reconciling with Zheling without the old letter or route chart, and any two Galebreaker pieces make you quicker on bridges. The Overlook Stargazer's lens helm is under "Starworks Gear".

### The Seven Pieces

- **Old Mailbag** (backpack): quality 4, pack capacity +4, item ID 500093
- **Rainhush Earmuffs** (headset): quality 4, Hearing +0.5, durability 40, item ID 500097
- **Mossgauze Mask** (face mask): quality 4, Head Armor +1, durability 40, item ID 500098
- **Mirrorgrain Plate** (body armor): quality 5, Body Armor +2, durability 80, item ID 500099
- **Galebreaker Hood** (helmet): quality 4, Head Armor +2, durability 50, item ID 500100
- **Galebreaker Mantle** (body armor): quality 4, Body Armor +2, durability 70, item ID 500101
- **Galebreaker Pack** (backpack): quality 4, pack capacity +5, item ID 500102
- Repairable: the five pieces with durability can be fixed at the workbench (the Old Mailbag and Galebreaker Pack have no durability)
- On death: they are not bound — fall during a raid and they stay behind with the rest of your gear

### How to Get It

- **The Waylayer** (chief, Upturned Post Hut): drops the Old Mailbag **30%** of the time, nothing the other 70%.
- **Rain Listener** (chief, Rainlisten Grotto): drops the Rainhush Earmuffs **30%** of the time, nothing the other 70%.
- **Gnat Piper** (chief, Frogsong Pool, **night only**): drops the Mossgauze Mask **30%** of the time, nothing the other 70%.
- **Mirror Guest** (chief, Mirrorwater Temple, **night only**): drops the Mirrorgrain Plate **30%** of the time, nothing the other 70%.
- **Galebreaker Ranger (Chaser / Stalker / Warden)** (chiefs, the three return relay platforms: K1 Hanging Root Wood, K2 Fallen Star Workshop, K3 Windsong Boardwalk): the Chaser wears the Galebreaker Mantle, the Stalker carries the Galebreaker Pack and the Warden wears the Galebreaker Hood; each leaves its own piece behind **40%** of the time, nothing the other 60%. Completing the set means beating all three.
- **Jeff's prelude (Ground Zero)**: the Galebreaker Warden guarding the lost navigation instrument uses the same profile and has a **40%** chance to drop the Galebreaker Hood.
- "Night" means 19:00 to 05:00 game time: by day you will only find followers at Frogsong Pool and Mirrorwater Temple, and the chief only comes out when you approach at night.
- The piece that drops comes at **full durability**.
- Never in any random pool (island crates, Wish Fountain, daily sign-in, boss loot crates) — these chiefs are the only source.

### Uses on the Isles

**Old Mailbag** (in the backpack slot):
- Carry it on the isles and, as long as there are letters you have not received, **the pigeons bring one extra letter that raid** (once per raid).

**Rainhush Earmuffs** (in the headset slot):
- Every chief's and island lord's warnings (rings on the ground, lunge lines, a tripwire pulling taut, the flute being played) **stay lit longer** — 1.25 times as long.
- The Rain Listener needs **16 of your shots** at the cave mouth to bring rocks down (8 without them).

**Mossgauze Mask** (in the face-mask slot):
- The cloud gnats around you cannot tell you are aiming at them and **stop dodging your shots**.

**Mirrorgrain Plate** (in the armor slot):
- Wear it to see Zheling — he knows the pattern: **you can reconcile without the old letter or the route chart**. Only what you are wearing when you see him counts.

**Galebreaker pair** (any two of the Galebreaker Hood / Galebreaker Mantle / Galebreaker Pack worn at once):
- You walk and run **12% faster** while standing on **bridges and relay platforms**; the bonus comes off as soon as you step off.

### Tips

**The Waylayer:**
- When you are within **2.5 m** and carrying island consumables, a ring lights at its feet and it reaches for **0.8 seconds**; if you are still within 2.9 m when the ring finishes, it snatches **your most valuable island consumable** (bento, salve, lantern, incense, charm, veil, gnat zapper or fan — one from the stack; keepsakes, materials and the game's own items are left alone). Then it flicks to the far end of the hut and runs **30% faster for 6 seconds**.
- Back off while the ring is lit and it grabs nothing. It snatches at most twice per fight.
- If it does snatch something, run it down: your things are on its body and land in its loot box when it falls.
- Below **40% health** it drops what it took at its feet and stops snatching.

**Rain Listener:**
- It counts the shots you fire **around the cave mouth (within 26 m of the cave rock)** — per trigger pull, so a shotgun blast counts once — and every **8 shots** it brings **2 rocks** down beside you (2.4 m radius, about 1.4 seconds of warning).
- Fire less at the mouth, switch to melee, or draw it out of the cave first, and the count stops rising.
- Once its Rainhush Earmuffs are crit through it can no longer hear your gunfire or bring rocks down.

**Gnat Piper:**
- When you come within 30 m it stands still and **plays its flute for 1 second** with a ring at its feet (the ring does no damage); then it calls cloud gnats to you, and **for 8 seconds every gnat on the map comes at you**, gnat zappers or not. It plays about every 9 seconds.
- Deal **8 damage** to it during that second and the tune breaks off.
- Once its Mossgauze Mask is crit through it can no longer play.

**Mirror Guest:**
- While you are **near the mirror pool (within 45 m)**, about every 8 seconds it lights a ring (1.8 m radius) **behind you**, at its own position mirrored across you, flips there **1.1 seconds** later and slams down, leaving a translucent **reflection** where it stood (20 health, fades after 6 seconds).
- Turn and step out as soon as a ring lights at your back. **Shatter the reflection first** and it reels for **3 seconds**, and does not flip while it reels.
- Move more than 45 m from the pool and it stops flipping.
- Once its Mirrorgrain Plate is at zero durability it still flips, but can no longer leave a reflection.

**Galebreaker Rangers (all three):**
- Stay inside 6 m for about **2.5 seconds** with its lunge ready and it retreats toward 9 m to seek another lunge. Hugging it does not permanently disable the move.
- When you are **6–28 m** away, a **lunge line** runs along the ground from its feet to just in front of you and a ring (1.8 m radius) lights at the landing spot; once the line is fully lit it dashes in, slams down and then **staggers for 1.2 seconds**. **Sidestep** as soon as the line lights.
- Their whole group is **no friend of the scavengers** and fights them on sight — lead one over and let them go at each other.
- **Chaser (K1)**: lunges twice — first to just in front of you, then it re-aims at where you are now and lands just **behind** you for a second slam; sidestep both. Its line stays lit about 0.9 seconds, twice as long once its Galebreaker Mantle is at zero durability.
- **Stalker (K2)**: lunges once, then flicks back to where it started to shoot from the platform edge — do not chase it into a gunfight at the edge; wait for its next lunge. Below half health its pack comes apart and it stops flicking back.
- **Warden (K3)**: lunges only once and its line stays lit longest (about 1.2 seconds), the easiest of the three to read — learn the lunge on it first. Its line stays lit twice as long once its Galebreaker Hood is shot through.

[tip] All of them show a boss health bar and name and come back every raid even after you beat them; first kills are recorded in the archipelago journal under "About the isles → This run · journey progress". Talk to Fuzhou, Weibai, Qinghe or Miantai and they mention where the chiefs you have not beaten yet are and how to handle them.
