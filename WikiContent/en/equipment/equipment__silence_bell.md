## Hushbell Ward

The Bell Keeper scratched on the brass: It stays silent. It only rings when it takes a hit for you. Equipped, it wraps you in a bronze bell ward that refills on its own and takes enemy blows before your health does.

### Where to get it

Reach **affinity level 5** with the Silent Bell Keeper, then select **Bell Keeper's Bronze Ward** from the interaction group. Exchange **30 seashells** for one ward. It sits beside Bell Keeper's Keepsakes as the Keeper's second box. Only shells in your backpack count; take stored shells out first. Failed item delivery returns the shells.

The bell-court device offers the same Bronze Ward box with the Keeper's affinity requirement and seashell price, even when he is absent. The box follows the Keeper home after marriage, with the same item and price. Each exchange delivers one item; reopen the box to trade again. It is not sold by Dingdang, is excluded from random reward pools and is never dropped by enemies.

### How to use it

1. Equip it in a totem slot and enter any raid scene.
2. A thin bronze bar appears above your head. It shows the ward, which holds **80 points** when full.
3. When an enemy hits you, the damage lands on the ward first. Only what breaks through reaches your health.

**Visual & audio cues:**
- **Active ward**: a pale golden bell rotates above a turning foot seal while golden motes rise; its height tracks remaining capacity, flaring with a low hum and shrinking on each hit
- **Shattered**: the bell bursts outward from gold to vermilion with ink smoke and a deep toll, leaving only a dim ember rim
- **Reforging**: once recovery begins, the bell rebuilds ring by ring from rim to crown; at full charge it chimes a clear ding and the bar fades away

### Rules

- Only enemy attacks are absorbed, explosions included. Damage over time (burning, poison, bleeding and so on) and equipment effects go straight to your health.
- The ward takes damage after armor and resistances are applied, so your usual damage reduction still works.
- Refilling starts **5 seconds** after the last hit, at **20 points per second**. An empty ward refills in about 4 seconds.
- After shattering, it stays quiet for at least **9 seconds**. A hit during that time restarts the 5-second count from that hit, and whichever ends later wins.
- Unequipping keeps the ward as it is; equip it again to carry on. Swapping it off and on does not refill it.
- Death or changing scenes refills it.
- Protects only the main player and does nothing in the base. Copies sitting in a backpack, in storage or on an NPC do nothing.
- The bar is hidden while inventory, map or pause UI is open, and comes back afterwards.
- It can be worn with the Energy Shield. The Energy Shield heals back part of each frontal hit; the Hushbell Ward is a layer that takes hits first. They work independently.

### Attributes

- **Type**: Totem
- **Quality**: 5
- **Weight**: 0.4
- **Base value**: 18,000 (item valuation; exchange price is 30 seashells)

[tip] Sustained fire is what breaks the ward. Step behind cover for 5 seconds and it refills itself, which suits fights that come in waves.
